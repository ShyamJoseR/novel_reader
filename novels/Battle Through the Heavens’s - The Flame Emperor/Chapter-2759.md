"Fortunately, I caught up... Leave the rest to me."

The flames surged, and endless power began to emerge from his body. Xiao Yan smiled and said something to the people behind him.

"Xiao Yan?"

Xiao Yan knew that the strength of these three was not bad, and they had a lot of means.

They couldn't help but need to resist the purification and burning of the nine-color holy flame, or resist the repressive force of the Flame God Cauldron. Greatly increased... The breath of fear and death quietly poured into my heart.

It seems that the current Xiao Yan has grown to such a terrifying level that the three of them are helpless!
