# Chapter Vol 4 2652: Hidden enemy?

The Great Thousand World, the Human World, a desolate desert city.

In the dark brown desert, the winds are ups and downs, and there is a touch of despair. Dry, hot, is the whole picture here.

This place is almost an extremely remote area, located close to the edge of the human world and the cracks of the planes of the evil races outside the territory, and this is exactly the case, there is almost no eye line of any power infiltrating here.

In the desert, there is a small city made entirely of sand and stones.

There are desert Jedi all around, and the sky is occupied by the vicious beast Tianying, as if this is a forgotten country!

"So desolate?"

Xiao Yan was bathed in black robes, looking at this city in the desert, he couldn't help but wonder.

Those representatives representing the peak forces in the Great Thousand Worlds actually chose such a desolate place to meet?

Are they afraid of certain forces?

Or are they being watched by those transcendent forces, and they can only resort to this, in order to protect themselves?

"In the vast world, there are eyeliners from the center of the human world. In order to prevent some unnecessary troubles from being exposed during this trip, we can only choose to do it in such a remote place."

"I hope the domain master can understand."

Feng Lao shook his head, these things are obviously not something she can decide... This is the common decision of those guys.

After all, once this kind of hidden negotiation is discovered by the Human World Center, I am afraid that waiting for these superpowers will be a devastating blow.

That kind of painful price is not something they can afford!

Xiao Yan shook his head, such a hideous move was undoubtedly as dark as a mouse crossing the street, but there was no better way than this before it was strong enough to compete with this giant in the center of the human world.

Strength is always the best pass for this world!

"Elder Feng, lead the way."

Xiao Yan naturally knew the seriousness of the matter, and immediately shook his head slightly.

The brown hurricane began to move, and Xiao Yan and the two stepped into it first, heading towards their destination.

Just stepping into the city, there are few people in the city. At this time, in a quiet pavilion, there are already several figures waiting here quietly.

When Xiao Yan and the two stepped into the city, the powerhouses who were waiting here opened their eyes one after another, as if the people who were waiting had already arrived.

After a while, everyone smoothly converged.

Xiao Yan looked up and counted it carefully.

There were a total of seven people in front of them, with different expressions, but at this time their eyes were all locked on themselves. Although their strength was just at the level of fighting immortals, they represented the huge forces behind them.

Obviously, the oppression in the center of the human world is too strong, and these superpowers dare not show their true colors at all, so they can only resort to this.

"Everyone, this is the descendant of the Xiao clan, the Lord of the Xiao domain. If you have any concerns about the forces behind you, please discuss it here."

Feng Lao was the first to speak, and covered the entire body with an airtight energy barrier.

"Xiao Yuzhu, this trip is a secret, please show the clan marks."

When the seven people heard the words, their eyes became vigilant. They were all in danger, and they could only be more careful about such dangerous things.

"It really is the Xiao clan pattern."

Xiao Yan slowly stimulated the blood of the Xiao family in his body, revealing the lines of the family.

"We are all messengers of our own powers. If there is any offense for a while, please ask Lord Xiao Yu to show mercy."

As soon as the words fell, the atmosphere in the entire pavilion suddenly became strange.

"My Spirit-Swallowing Clan has been led by the Xiao Clan for generations. If the Domain Master Xiao has any need, he can just say it... When Feng Lao went there, many voices of resistance in the clan have now been completely suppressed by the clan leader."

One of the seven, the first to vote on his tough attitude.

The Spirit-Swallowing Clan and the Xiao Clan have a very close relationship, and even during these long years, they have been looking for traces of the Xiao Clan.

"…"

Xiao Yan nodded, his goodwill towards the Spirit Swallowing Clan kept rising.

The seven forces are all the existences with the background and power of the Great Thousand World. The strength of the ancestors or Xeons behind them is almost all over the seventh-level reincarnation level or even the eighth level. They have their support, and now the endless flame alliance's strength. With the background, you have the qualification to step into the underworld and start the plan to save the Xiao family!

This is also the reason why Xiao Yan will choose to go to the appointment.

"Hehe, we can't compare to the spirit swallowing clan."

"The position of the Human World Center and Daqiangong is unclear now, and even the many powerhouses who obliterated the Xiao family were hidden in the dark. If they jumped out and joined the Endless Flame Alliance at this time, they would undoubtedly become the thorns in the eyes of many powerhouses, and let us pass on for countless years. The foundation is destroyed once..."

"I hope Lord Xiao understands."

Among the crowd, a woman spoke slowly.

"I understand, you can speak up about all your concerns."

"Today is the best time to discuss matters."

Xiao Yan was not in a hurry, because he knew that the Human World Center's actions were too arrogant and domineering, which had already caused dissatisfaction from countless forces.

All you need to do now is to take the lead.

"The matter now is to save the crown of Chang'e, the crown of Yao Ji and other strong men who are imprisoned and sealed in the town of demons. Once these big figures show up and declare their support for the Endless Flame Alliance, we will naturally follow."

"At that time, even the center of the human world and those powerhouses who are hidden in the dark will be afraid of us by three points."

After discussing for a long time, they only came up with some illusory verbal promises. After all, this kind of standing in the team is no trivial matter.

"I know what you mean, please tell them, we will do these things naturally, but I just hope that when the time comes... don't forget your original intention."

"Let them wait for the good news."

Xiao Yan shrugged slightly. Obviously, the joining of these forces will inevitably lead to leaders... and now that the enemy is unknown, Xiao Yan will not be stupid enough to announce the sleeping place of the strong Xiao family.

"Since that's the case, then I'll wait for the good news from Lord Xiao Yu."

The few people looked at each other, obviously this discussion was very pleasant, and they did not expect Xiao Yan to be so easy to talk.

"Everyone wait...Xiao Yuzhu, I have a request."

Not long after, when the discussion was over and they were about to leave here, a figure who had been silent in the crowd suddenly stood up, and there seemed to be a dark frenzy in his deep eyes.

"What's the matter?"

A lot of power in Xiao Yan's body began to roll little by little, and it seemed that he could smell some terrifying aura from the body of this only fighting immortal-level powerhouse in front of him!

"I want to invite Xiao Yuzhu to go somewhere for a walk The name is: Hell."

"Boom!"

Before this guy finished speaking, his entire body exploded, and a pool of pitch-black blood gushed out from his body, completely isolating the entire city!

After a while, the whole wooden house began to sink, and it seemed to be rushing towards some strange place.

"What did you do?"

"We're just in charge of spreading the word!"

"You actually want to kill us?"

Fear occupied the hearts of the other six people little by little. They never imagined that there were traitors among the seven representatives.
