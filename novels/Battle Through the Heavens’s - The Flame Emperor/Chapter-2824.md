# Chapter Vol 4 2768: Inside the Dragon's Tomb

As the battle outside the Dragon Tomb came to an end, some unknown things were also happening inside the Dragon Tomb.

In the silent and ancient dragon tomb, the smell of time permeates everywhere, and there are many colorful keel corpses hanging in the void of the dragon tomb out of thin air. Obviously, these are the strong dragons who were buried in the dragon tomb after their fall.

Among them, the weakest one had reached the reincarnation in strength before his death. Such a huge number is also in line with the dragon clan's detached position as the overlord of heaven and earth in the Great Thousand World.

Countless keels were placed in the dragon tomb in a disorderly manner, but their heads and bodies were uniformly facing a hazy area in the dragon tomb, which was extremely respected.

It was an open area suspended in the sky of the Dragon Tomb, at the core of the Dragon Tomb, the Dragon God Square.

The Longshen Square is very large, about a kilometer in size. However, there are not many decorations on the square. There is only a very simple lotus stone platform. On the stone platform sits an ancient stone statue that has been buried for a long time. .

The body of the stone statue is the same as that of an ordinary human being, and it is even much thinner than a normal human being. The whole body is not too brilliant. Only the whole body of the stone statue has an inexplicable and powerful golden pattern flowing.

There seems to be some transcendent power in the golden lines, and even through the lines, you can vaguely see some objects like perfect jade inside the stone statue surging slightly... but those seem to be illusory, like illusory shadows.

In front of the stone statue, there is an inconspicuous golden stone tablet. The golden stone tablet is like an ancient object standing in front of the stone statue... Standing here, there seems to be an illusion. The picture inside the stone statue seems to be caused by the projection of the stone tablet. ?

At this time, there were several figures around the square, and everyone looked at the stone monument and stone statue with serious eyes.

There was a dignified look in his eyes, and he was obviously worried about something.

Among them, the leader has bright eyes, and his blond hair adds endless majesty to him. At a glance, he has the demeanor of a king of the overlord of heaven and earth.

What's even more peculiar is that there are thick golden scales on his body, and the scales are flashing with magnificent colors. Even if the real powerhouse of the Nine Reincarnation Rank sees it, I am afraid they will not be able to start.

There was an unquestionable majesty in the latter's old eyes, and the eyes were full of ferocity and rage.

Beside him, there was an old man in white robe with a kind face, and a man who looked very young walking back and forth, stagnant. The strength of the two was strong and weak.

"Although the dragon clan civil strife has been going on for a long time, the silver dragon king is also a bit wise, and he did not disturb too much of the power of the dragon clan. The battle only covered the nine dragon kings... Now we secretly step into the dragon tomb, and the dragon king and the fire dragon king are under the control of the outside world. , I am afraid it will not be the opponent of the Silver Dragon King and others."

The dead silence didn't last long, and the voice of the young shadow was the first to break the silence.

"..."

The voice of the young shadow fell, and there was no response from the two of them. They naturally knew the importance of this trip.

"Now, I can only give it a go. I hope that the Silver Dragon King will not commit the sin of killing his fellow clan."

"But...Dragon Lord, are you really going to do this?"

"Although this approach is feasible, the risk is too great."

After a moment of hesitation, the hoarse voice of the white-robed old man echoed out, and the worry contained in it could not be added.

"My origin dragon body was severely damaged in the first World War, and then when it was hidden in the final passage of Dou Di Continent and Dou Qi World, it was completely destroyed by the powerhouses of the evil race from outside the territory, which caused my current strength to suffer too much damage. too serious."

"If we don't take this step, I'm afraid that with my power, it will be difficult to completely quell the civil strife in the Dragon Race..."

The leader is arrogant, and his conversation is extremely extraordinary. Combined with such great courage, who else is there in the entire dragon family except the legendary Golden Dragon King?

However, the Golden Dragon King at this time, both in terms of strength and mentality, was strikingly different from the Golden Dragon King who was living in seclusion in Xiao Yan's body.

Even so, as the latter stared at the ancient stone monument on the square in front of him, a wave of powerless fluctuations spread from the depths of his soul... There is a legacy of the power of the Dragon God!

Looking at the huge dragon clan, there are very few people who are qualified to step into it, but in countless reincarnations, no one who steps into it survives.

No one knows what the mystery is in this stele, and whether there really is the power of the Dragon God, but this does not affect its lofty status in the slightest.

"Dragon Lord, your body is damaged, and now your strength is only nine-fold reincarnation. On the other hand, the Silver Dragon King's servant, in the days when you left, secretly enjoyed many rare resources of the Dragon Clan alone, but his power has vaguely touched the peak state. If this stalemate continues, I am afraid that the situation on our side will be extremely unfavorable..."

"Your decision is correct."

After the white-robed old man beside him hesitated for a while, he could only sigh, and then agreed with the Golden Dragon King's approach.

Up to now, I am afraid that this is the only way, but the Golden Dragon King is the supreme leader of the entire dragon clan. If the latter has any accident, I am afraid it will make the already civil strife of the dragon clan even worse.

"I entered the stone tablet and realized the power of the Dragon God. I will try my best to compress the time to the shortest possible time. If the external situation cannot be reversed, you can temporarily surrender... During this period, remember not to act recklessly, otherwise everything we do will be It will be a waste of time.”

There was something in the words of the Golden Dragon King, and he seemed to be looking at the two of them worriedly.

"Yes, Dragon Lord."

Following the Golden Dragon King's request, the two looked at each other and clasped their fists respectfully.

The worry of the Golden Dragon King is nothing more than the fact that the Baxia Dragon King and others tried to break the net with the Silver Dragon King, so they left their entrustment.

"If something happens to me... I will take my Golden Dragon King's holy decree and go to the Endless Flame Alliance to invite Xiao Yan, and the dragon clan will be handed over to him, I can rest assured."

After a while, the Golden Dragon King looked at the end of the endless void of the Dragon Tomb, and suddenly there was a trace of golden light flickering, and there was even a faint aura of Xiao Yan, which was also moving.

How could Xiao Yan's breath appear here?

"The dragon master has obtained the true inheritance of the dragon god, and such a situation will definitely not occur."

The old man in white robe shuddered when he heard the words, and then said with difficulty.

These words are more a persuasion to himself than a praise for the Golden Dragon King.

"Follow your orders, Dragon Lord."

The young man on the side nodded solemnly, and naturally knew the importance of this trip. Now, only the Golden Dragon King is the only way to break the boat. Otherwise, once the dragon clan civil strife is prolonged, it will be extremely unfavorable to the Golden Dragon King's lineage!

"Long Yi, your talent and understanding are extraordinary... After this time, I will make you the tenth king of the dragon clan."

The Golden Dragon King turned his head, his pupils were full of relief, who would have thought that Xiao Yan's titular eldest brother, although his strength was not strong, his mind and head were above ordinary people.

"By the way... Still haven't found the traces of Long Qing, Long Hao, and the three leaders of the Sacred Armored Dragon Army?"

After explaining it, the Golden Dragon King also asked the old man in white robe, that these important things naturally need the old man in white robe to know.
