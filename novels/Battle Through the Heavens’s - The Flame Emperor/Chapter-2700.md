# Chapter Vol 4 2648: 8 Winged Seraph, Heavenly Fire Holy Inquisition

"A man was born between heaven and earth... Who can stop me?"

Facing the attack of the two powerhouses of the eighth-level reincarnation, the demon emperor was unmoved, snorted coldly, and suddenly pulled out the domineering demon spear behind him.

At a point in the sky, the shadows of countless birds and beasts tilted down from it, carrying a supreme demon power that was extremely domineering, causing the whole world to tremble faintly.

Even if he suffers from a dark disease... so what?

Can you stop yourself with a half-baked eight-fold reincarnation level and a weird eight-fold reincarnation level?

Ridiculous!

"Is this a battle between the strongest?"

Countless strong people watched the battle in the sky, and the idea of ​​becoming stronger in their hearts became deeper and deeper.

After a short fight, on the sky, the figure of the demon emperor began to become embarrassed, and he didn't know whether it was intentional or unintentional... In a battle of that level, two evil powerhouses had the upper hand!

"All the powerhouses above the reincarnation rank, prepare to help the demon emperor with me!"

Yun Yun's pretty face is cold, and the number of powerhouses such as the demigods around is too large. If the Demon Emperor is defeated, the entire Endless Fire Territory will not escape bad luck.

"Demon Emperor... As strong as you are, there will be a day when you will get hurt."

"Your life will be ended by our own hands!"

The sarcastic voice fell slowly with a burst of evil light.

"Yes?"

"Look at the top of your head... what is that?"

At this moment, there was a malicious smile on the corner of the Demon Emperor's mouth. It seemed that the battle just now was just to delay time?

"Don't be alarmist, today, you..."

"Wait... what kind of fluctuation is this?"

Finally, the Fourth Evil God and Mortuo gave a random look and were about to completely obliterate the Demon Emperor, but when they saw the top of this piece of heaven and earth, some sacred ray of light began to pave the way!

The sacred power that was filled in it turned out to be so moved by the power in the two of them that they became afraid!

Above the sky of the Endless Fire Territory, circles of pure white halos began to appear, like energy ripples suddenly rippling from the center of the lake, taking the hole in the sky where the demon emperor descended as a gap, and spread out neatly.

At the moment when the halo was rippling, there were actually pieces of white feathers scattered randomly, and the power full of them actually caused this swaying endless fire field to become temporarily quiet.

It seems like some kind of supreme divine power that cannot be destroyed by heaven and earth, which is admirable!

"This is……"

At this moment, Yun Yun and the others, who were on the verge of dying, suddenly shrank their eyes, as if they suddenly noticed something, and their heart beat became more intense.

In the gap in the sky, the pure white halo and feathers poured out, causing the many evil auras that attacked the endless fire zone to retreat rapidly, as if there was some kind of terrifying existence that could destroy them at will!

Not long after, layers of light circles rose, and five graceful figures appeared high above the heads of the three demon emperors!

The pure white wings stretched out gently, and the wings that had overlapped several times were filled with hatred and disdain for evil forces!

Five angels!

Among the five, the leader has reached a whole new level in terms of beauty and strength, and the wings behind him are even more monstrous purple!

Extremely noble, shining like the holy fire of dawn!

Purple, eight-winged seraph, half-step eight-fold reincarnation level strength!

"God... an angel?"

At this moment, the fourth evil **** and Mortuo looked at each other at the same time, looking at the shadows of the five angels entrenched in the sky, the pride in their hearts disappeared, but a fear gradually took over their hearts.

"It's finally here... I thought you were lost."

Seeing the arrival of these powerhouses, the demon emperor laughed, and the pressure on his body was slightly reduced.

"what?"

The demon emperor said such a word, but suddenly set off countless stormy waves!

These five angels... came here to kill the Demon Emperor?

"Demon Emperor, you trespassed into the forbidden area of ​​our angel family without authorization, and even injured several wise angels. The kingdom of angels will never let you go."

"But before that, we have more important things to do."

The leader's voice just finished speaking, and his clear eyes directly locked on the fourth evil **** and Mo Tuo in front of him.

The kingdom of angels is a supreme existence that stands on the opposite side of evil forces, even demonic forces. In their eyes, the priority of evil forces is above everything else!

"what?"

The fourth evil **** beside him, Mo Tuo, Yun Yun and others heard the words, and they were all shocked in their minds.

Where is the kingdom of angels?

The demon emperor actually broke into it, and even wounded a number of wise angels and retreated?

The strength of the demon emperor, didn't it just break through the seventh reincarnation level?

There is no doubt about the strength of the kingdom of angels, among which there is a real nine-fold reincarnation order!

What's the mystery in this...?

"It's just to investigate some more obscure matters."

The Demon Emperor's voice was cold, but anyone could clearly hear the hatred for the kingdom of angels in his words.

"This account will be calculated later."

"Yeah, sooner or later it will be completely clear."

"With your strength, it's still impossible to deal with two eight-layer reincarnation-level powerhouses at the same time... Why don't we join forces?"

Although the grievances between the two were not small, the demon emperor temporarily put aside his prejudice against the kingdom of angels when the evil race from outside the territory was in power.

After all, the strength of the five angels, the strongest, is only half a step and eighth-level, and the other strengths are hovering around the sixth-level reincarnation level.

"unnecessary!"

Facing the demon emperor's good intentions, the first angel roared arrogantly, a cold voice emerged, the boundless purple angel wings began to wave, and the entire sky was stirred up in an instant, as if there was some kind of angel power, and began to sway. mobilize, gather.

The kingdom of angels has always acted vigorously and resolutely. Since they have encountered the fourth evil **** here, they will not let the latter ignore it!

"What an annoying bunch of guys."

The void around the fourth evil **** was imprisoned by the power of angels, and he naturally knew that all this seemed to be the premeditated plan of the demon emperor...

"boom!"

The demon emperor was the first to fight with Mo Tuo, and there seemed to be some mysterious imprints in Mo Tuo's body, of course, it could not be completely obliterated by the angel.

However, in the fight just now, the demon emperor seemed to have retained his strength. Now that the two fought again, Mo Tuo was actually defeated by the demon emperor!

"hateful!!!"

The fourth evil spirit was relieved, and when he faced the five angels in front of him, a burst of fear could not help but emerge.

Four seven-winged wise angels, and even a seraph with eight purple wings!

Such a luxurious lineup, even if it is difficult to deal with Mo Tuo who has recovered his peak power... After all, the power of angels in the kingdom of angels is not a joke.

"Angel Judgement!"

Without any hesitation, the five angels lined up in unison, and began to sing the angelic incantation exclusive to the kingdom of angels.

Countless figures raised their heads and looked towards the sky at the same time, and suddenly found that pure white filaments of light were rising from the void of the endless fire domain!

Those demon clouds that contain the countless evil forces of the Fourth Evil God inhabit the countless evil auras that are easily penetrated by them and hunted down!

Countless filaments of light destroyed all the demon clouds little by little, and finally restored the periphery of the Endless Fire Territory to normal, but they began to gather at the end of this world!

In just a moment, the countless evil armies led by the fourth evil **** were all destroyed.

Is this the supreme power of the kingdom of angels?

This is... the power of the eight-winged seraph?

Only eight wings are strong to this point, if it is ten wings...or the legendary twelve wings, what kind of power to destroy the world.

I am afraid that even the real nine-layer reincarnation-level powerhouse can't compete with it...
