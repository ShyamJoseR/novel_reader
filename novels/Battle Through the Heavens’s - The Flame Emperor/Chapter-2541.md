# Chapter Vol 4 2489: Loose Heavenly Dao Reincarnation Flame!

"What exactly is going on?"

The blue dragon scales on Xiao Yan's body suddenly stood upright, and a wave of unease and fear filled his heart.

"Hey!"

The blood mist trembled, a pale figure poured out, and the blood mist remaining around him was instantly evaporated by a touch of orange-gold flamingo.

"The flame of the reincarnation of the heavens?"

At this moment, the blue dragon scales all over Xiao Yan's body emitted a humming meaning, and even the holy flames in his body also burst out with an unprecedented sense of pride.

The fire of holy flame is resisting, telling endless madness!

Gathering the power of the six holy flames, even if it is not comparable to the third holy flame, but as the noble pride of holy flames, how could they lower their heads and beg for mercy on their own accord!

"Bang bang bang!"

The blood mist was evaporated, and the eyes of the other six people on the scene were immediately condensed. Although they were entangled by many Huo slaves, none of them directly killed the Huo slaves with such thunderous means as Xiao Yan.

"This Xiao Yan's strength should not be underestimated, but I'm afraid there will be a good show to watch next."

The remaining four slowly dealt with their respective fire slaves, and immediately looked at Xiao Yan with a look of schadenfreude.

The fusion-type Huo Slave, which contains the power of the holy flame of the reincarnation of the heavens, is comparable to the human five-fold reincarnation level, and such a terrifying existence is enough to really kill Xiao Yan here.

"Wang, I will take care of these guys, and that guy, it's just a pity that the two holy flame controllers, gathering the holy flames in their bodies, should be able to imprison that guy for a while."

Tiandao Samsara Yan said respectfully to the man on the throne, and then his eyes locked directly on Xiao Yan.

It seems that only he knows everything here.

Outsiders only know that the Six Paths of Reincarnation is a peerless treasure land containing holy flames, but whether it is a treasure land or an abyss, I am afraid it is still unknown.

Tiandao Samsara Yan lowered his head indifferently, and said in an icy voice, "Slave Yan, do it!"

"Destroy him!"

A strange voice sounded, and immediately there was a flash of orange-gold fire in the eyes of Yan Nu in front of Xiao Yan.

"The Battle of Holy Flame, I really miss it."

Xiao Yan smiled slightly, the corners of his mouth raised a strange and crazy arc, and the glazed holy flames swirled around him with a six-color halo, which was incomparably gorgeous.

"Intruder, die!"

When Yan Nu uttered the last word, a huge sound that shook his heart and soul swept out, carrying some kind of strange wave that could not be recognized by the naked eye.

"Soul storm."

Xiao Yan's face was not afraid, he dominated the dragon scales to protect his body, and the six-color holy flames circulated. In coordination with the surging of the seven-layered spiritual palace in his body, a mighty wave of hundreds of feet suddenly spewed out in the void!

The invisible soul power swept through, and the terrifying soul power rippling in the mind was like some invisible hurricane, and even Tang Feng and the four couldn't help but turn cold.

"This Xiao Yan's strength is actually so powerful?"

Youhai Dragon Snake knocked back the Huo Nu in front of him with one palm, and immediately looked at Xiao Yan who was fighting against the extremely powerful Yan Nu in disbelief.

The soul power of the Immortal Ascension Boundary, if there is not enough opportunity, I am afraid that it will not be able to reach it in a lifetime.

The overwhelming soul storm gushed out, like an invisible hurricane whizzing past, and finally abruptly fought in mid-air, shaking the entire void of the Scarlet Fire Golden Realm completely.

One after another giant waves of souls rolled out, and the terrifying fluctuations shook the entire space to the brink, as if all the power was overshadowed by the battle of this level of souls.

"Heaven's Way of Reincarnation Points!"

At the moment when this soul confrontation just fell, countless energies gathered in the direction of Yan Nu above the entire void, where the space suddenly tightened like a heart, and immediately turned into the most dazzling flame and fireworks bloomed out.

In the center of the void, Yan Nu's palm suddenly lifted, and he quietly pointed at Xiao Yan. There were countless orange-gold fire patterns lingering on his fingertips, and there was even some kind of destructive power underneath.

"Top Immortal Arts?"

At this moment, Xiao Yan's mind reverberated with some kind of heart palpitations, it seemed that under Yan Nu's finger, all things died out!

That is the top immortal art with the blessing of the third holy flame, and its power is almost as powerful as it is outrageous!

"Tai Chi Heavenly Emperor Slash!"

Gu Fangqian's shadow flickered, and a silver sword gushed out, drawing a beautiful arc in the void, and the divine and ferocious aura crawls out like a peerless beast.

"Bang!"

One finger and one sword just collided, and suddenly bursts of harsh light flashed across the entire Scarlet Fire Golden Realm. The bodies of the five people around them quickly retreated, and no one dared to be contaminated with that special power.

"You are crazy!!!"

Xiao Yan's eyes flashed with extreme compassion and love, and he embraced Yun Yun's delicate body in his arms. Although his face was serious, his voice was very gentle.

At this time, Yun Yun's splendid dress had already been torn apart by Yan Nu's finger, and a large piece of snow-white color was faintly revealed, and the whole person looked a little embarrassed.

Even with the Taiji Heavenly Emperor Sword as a guide, against a finger whose strength is comparable to that of a five-layered reincarnation rank Yan slave, he was almost seriously injured!

"Cough cough...I..."

"I just want to help you."

Yunyun's chest rose and fell violently, as if she wanted to forcibly cheer up, but after struggling, she quietly crawls on Xiao Yan's chest.

"Stay here."

"Netherworld Dragon Snake, please take care of me."

After Xiao Yan finished speaking, there was a madness in his eyes.

I can't keep my hands. The power in the Scarlet Fire Golden Realm is already devouring their little power of reincarnation. If they are stuck like this, I'm afraid everyone will stay here completely!

"Are you finally ready to use all your strength?"

The other four looked at Xiao Yan in anger, and immediately narrowed their eyes, staring at Xiao Yan intently.

"Since you are a slave of flames, let's see whose flame is more powerful!"

A cold arc appeared on Xiao Yan's face, his body was suspended in mid-air, and his palm suddenly slammed into the void.

I don't know if it was caused by soul power or other forces. The huge void in the Red Fire Golden Realm exploded abruptly, and scenes of colorful energy liquid flowed out.

The six colors of liquid, like six completely different flame slugs, just emerged, and the temperature in the entire hall dropped to the lowest point!

"These...are all holy flames?"

Among the four, someone kept speaking out The greed in their eyes was slowly replaced by shock.

The power of the holy flame is rampant, and it is extremely difficult to find traces. This Xiao Yan has so many holy flames in his body at the same time?

"Six Sacred Flames... It's really an out-and-out monster."

"It's a pity that there is that guy in the cave. This time, I'm afraid we can only destroy this place together."

Looking at the source of the six-color holy flame lava flowing around Xiao Yan's body, the irony in Tiandao Samsarayan's eyes slowly disappeared, and he turned to look at the ancient figure beside him, his eyes filled with regret.

In front of Xiao Yan, at the chest, a small cauldron of flame that looked ordinary began to emerge. Under the operation of Xiao Yan's burning power, it slowly rose, blooming with an endless brilliance that did not belong to this world.

The six holy flames slowly flowed into the small flame cauldron, and a splendid river of flames swept out. At the moment when it poured out, the entire hall seemed to be stabbed by some kind of sting. The gloomy black air slowly infiltrated from the depths of the triangular ruins.

The black air was icy and gloomy, and just after it emerged, several people present felt a strange emotional fluctuation.

That kind of fluctuation is a taste from outside the realm!
