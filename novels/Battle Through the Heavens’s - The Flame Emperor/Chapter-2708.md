With the blessing of the huge water attribute power all over his body, such a strong posture, his attack seems to be enough to make any strong person feel embarrassed.

The grey clothed figure rolled his palm, suddenly lifted it out of thin air, and quietly pressed down on Xiao Yan's side.

At the same time, an extremely powerful aura suddenly erupted from the surrounding sea area. Countless energy water columns that had been blessed by special powers spewed out, intersecting in the sky, vaguely forming a series of water cages.

But no matter how dangerous the waves were, they could never cause any real harm to Xiao Yan!

"Huhu... let's fight quickly."

Considering the peculiar nature of the void here, Xiao Yan still decided to end this place with the power of thunder. As for what was waiting for him, it was not something he should be concerned about.
