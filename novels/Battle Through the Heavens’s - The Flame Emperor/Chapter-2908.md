# Chapter Vol 4 2849: Love is forever!

On the top of the endless sky, Xiao Tianming raised his shriveled palm and looked at the tearful Thunder Lord below with a naive smile, his eyes full of pampering.

"I have no regrets in my life... Go back, this is not the place you should come."

Looking at the bright smile on Xiao Tianming's face, Lord Lei couldn't help smiling contentedly. The memory seemed to return to the first time we met.

This taciturn, but black-bellied guy, now it seems that people really love and hate.

"..."

Faced with Lord Lei's exhortation, Xiao Tianming did not answer, but his eyes suddenly became serious, like a solemn emperor who could not be desecrated, his eyes silently scanning all the creatures here.

"The current situation is basically a conspiracy, a conspiracy of the Slaughter Angel against you and against the Xiao Clan."

"You will die... I don't want you to die."

It's not worth it... There's no need to go to war for yourself and the kingdom of angels.

Once Xiao Tianming and the kingdom of angels start a complete war, such a scene will be enough to destroy the world, and the ultimate winner will undoubtedly be the evil race from outside the realm... The second world war is coming soon, if this internal battle consumes too much top power, this The world of Pian Daqian may be in some kind of dire situation.

It is already a very happy thing that I can meet Xiao Tianming before I die and fulfill my long-cherished wish.

"I won't go."

"I'm not leaving you anymore."

Xiao Tianming laughed wildly, and then suddenly there was endless power rising from his entire body, just like the sky of this sacred mountain range, there is a magnificent orange-gold volcano that will erupt with endless anger at any time, and the momentum is appalling.

"..."

Countless people looked at this genius of the Xiao clan who would rather give up everything for the sake of a girl, and they felt awe in their hearts, especially the heads of power who were like grass on the wall. They were even more ashamed.

Some people are willing to live with dirty masks and ugly bodies for a little bit of profit, while some people are just as they are, and would rather burn everything for the love in their hearts!

Remaining in the void, the power of the angels in the air began to disappear under the influence of its breath, and the entire peaceful sacred mountain range was filled with hot reincarnation ripples, as if a certain flame king had descended here.

"This fluctuation... is the power of holy flame!"

Feeling that the angelic power that had been carefully prepared to deal with Xiao Tianming was so easily eliminated by the opponent, the figures within the five towers on the pentagram array couldn't help but lose their voices.

"It's actually the fluctuation of the third holy flame, it's really surprising..."

The Slaughter Angel's beautiful eyes were filled with a look of surprise. Looking at the holy flame volcano rising from Xiao Tianming's body, a more arrogant smile appeared instead.

"Today is the day when my kingdom of angels will judge heresy."

"As a member of the Xiao family, what you are doing now..."

The smile on the corner of the Killing Angel's mouth was even more contemptuous, and then the high-pitched voice resounded through every corner of the Holy Inquisition Mountains.

He is still too young!

Even though his strength has reached the peak of a demigod, does he really think that he can fight against the entire kingdom of angels by himself?

"Hahaha."

"Killing Angels, why put so many charges on this God?"

"My people from the Xiao family are doing things, but it's not your turn to be a bereaved dog to intervene."

"Just because you want to get your hands on the Xiao clan... you don't deserve it."

"Besides... I am now a genius of the Xiao clan. Today's Xiao Tianming is just a common man who is obsessed with love."

Just as Killing Angel was halfway through, Xiao Tianming smiled casually, with an unquestionably arrogant tone in his smile.

"You do not deserve……"

The majestic voice spread to every corner of this world, and that shocking tone undoubtedly made the atmosphere of this world dry to the extreme, and it ignited instantly!

"Angel's Sanctuary, line up!"

Feeling the arrogance in Xiao Tianming's chest, the Killing Angel spoke again.

"Holy Mansion!"

"Holy Mansion!!!"

The mighty voices emerged from the mouths of countless strong angels around the Holy Trial Mountains, and countless pure white filaments poured out from their delicate bodies, and immediately injected them all into the altar!

The Holy Inquisition altar was filled with crystal clear white spar, suddenly being poured out by such a vast divine power, the entire altar suddenly rose up and suddenly floated in the air.

"This is……"

Among the crowd, there are many strong people with extensive experience, looking at this altar that rises into the void, their eyelids are beating violently.

"Angel's Sanctuary?"

"The God-hunting Light Array that claims to be able to judge everything in the world?"

……

Suddenly, a colorless and transparent energy light chain scattered from each of the five towers in the void, vaguely connecting the entire altar.

In the center of the altar, above the cross beam of light that bound the Thunder Lord, suddenly a surging holy light spewed out, and immediately turned into extremely pure white crystals.

Looking at the sacred and mysterious white crystal blocks, the Thunder Master's eyes became dignified. From there, the Thunder Master clearly felt a wave that was close to destruction.

"Pfft."

Before the Thunder Master could react, hundreds of spar stones surrounded him, and they immediately trembled together, as if they were constructing some void world.

The void in the altar began to become distorted and illusory. A round of dazzling white light holes did not know where to lead, but at this time, it was like a big mouth of blood, easily swallowing the Thunder Lord away.

Around the altar, countless ancient holy formations of angels are looming, and several seraphs and archangels sang longer angelic spells together.

"Xiao Tianming, go!"

The moment the Thunder Lord disappeared and was pulled into the corona vortex, he could clearly feel that he was leaving this world.

"Is this your trust?"

Xiao Tianming stepped slightly, no matter what the killing angel did today, he would use his own hands to destroy her in person.

"The Sanctuary of Angels, that's an existence that even gods can hunt downYou..."

The Killing Angel slowly floated outside the Light Array altar, and then stared at Xiao Tianming with contempt.

The reason why the kingdom of angels has been able to maintain a transcendent status in the great world is not only because they control the resurrection magic... but also because of countless divine powers such as judgment and sanctions.

With the power of judgment and sanction led by the power of angels, in the entire Great Thousand World, I am afraid that few people can escape.

"maybe."

Countless angelic divine powers, like sacred hot flames, slammed on Xiao Tianming's face, even if he was temporarily carrying the third holy flame, the orange-gold flame burst out with a strong fear!

Without the divine holy flame, how can it resist the coercion of such a god-hunting light formation?

"I hope that your current power can be powerful enough to subvert everything..."

Xiao Tianming's eyes began to change, and orange-gold flames instantly filled his eyes, skin, and flesh. The vast energy seemed to be able to easily crush the world.

Immediately, without any hesitation, he stood up suddenly and dived from the sky above the vortex at the center of the altar light array.

"Right is supreme, but..."

"Love lasts forever."
