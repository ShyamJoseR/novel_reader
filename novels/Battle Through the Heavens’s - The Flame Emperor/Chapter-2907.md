Let the Holy Thunder Clan become an existence that surpasses the Xiao Clan.

In the ensuing time, under the cultivation of the resources of their respective races, the two of them cultivated rapidly and looked forward to the next duel.

Ten years later, the younger generation of the Great Thousand World gathered together, led by super races such as the Xiao Clan and the Holy Thunder Clan, and went deep into the hinterland outside the territory to conduct life and death experiences.

The core information of that experience was leaked, and many young geniuses and powerhouses died in a tragic death outside the realm. Only Lei Zhu and Xiao Tianming helped each other, step by step, and only escaped from birth. Since then, the tacit understanding between the two has been good. He seemed a little worried that this grand event, which had exhausted all the power of the kingdom of angels and carefully arranged, would return without success.

In that case, the damage to the kingdom of angels would be too great.

"What do you say?"

Killing Angel shook his head slightly, and then a playful smile suddenly appeared on the corner of his mouth.

"Finally... it's here."

The Slaughter Angel's eyes slowly looked towards the sky above the altar of the Holy Inquisition, and a bunch of dazzling orange-gold flames slowly rose, from faint to bright, from inconspicuous to extremely brilliant.

That wave was like the arrival of an angry god's mansion, and the divine might was so great that it moved the breath of countless sacred mountains.

That wave of fluctuations can be confirmed without any further induction. That is the strongman of the Xiao family, Xiao Tianming, at the peak level of a demigod!

He is the real peak powerhouse in this vast world!

"Xiao...Xiao Tianming."

"Alas, they still can't hold their breath after all."

Although the Xiao Clan's illustrious reputation is vivid in our eyes, the ruthless means of killing the angels are obvious to all those who are present.

With the strength of one person, how can it compete with the entire kingdom of angels?

Xiao Tianming set foot alone in the holy city of the angel kingdom, in the sky of the Holy Trial Mountains, through the endless void in those deep eyes, suddenly locked on the weak shadow above the cross beam of light, looking at the cross beam of light emanating from above. The divine aura was eating away at Lei Master's life little by little. On Xiao Tianming's calm face, there were clearly bursts of murderous intent.

Many times, Xiao Tianming doesn't know what love is, but it is precisely because of the existence of Thunder Lord that Xiao Tianming is constantly motivated, making the latter continue to turn cocoons into butterflies, and eventually become a true genius in the Xiao family.

Looking at the beautiful figure in front of him, scenes of unforgettable memories poured into my heart like spring water.

How can these pedantic people understand what they have experienced in the near future?

"You shouldn't have come."

Lord Lei was surprised to know that when Xiao Tianming came to the meeting alone, he did not personally commit the feud of extermination of the clan, and he had a different temperament in it.
