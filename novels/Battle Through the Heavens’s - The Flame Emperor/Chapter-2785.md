# Chapter Vol 4 2730: Water curtain, rotten world!

"The Shuizhi Holy Throne thinks too highly of itself."

Xiao Yan chuckled lightly, and the many sources of holy flames on his hands that were obtained from the three women jumped at will, turning into beams of flames of light, killing them directly!

Now in Xiao Yan's hands, the number of holy flames has almost reached seventeen, and with the latter's Fen Jue and the eighth-layer Samsara strength, the combat power has reached a heart-pounding level!

Nine down, almost invincible!

"Boom!"

Countless dark and evil water columns slanted out of the Tianhe River, and blasted away at the three tombs of reincarnation, with an imposing momentum.

"Pfft!"

Even if Xiao Yan urged the source of the holy flame to strike with all his strength, trying to intercept the latter's attack, when the holy flame came into contact with the evil water column, it penetrated strangely and could not be intercepted.

"not good!"

Seeing such a strange scene, Xiao Yan's heart was horrified, the speed skyrocketed, and the brilliance of the holy flames around him became more and more dazzling!

Once the three tombs of reincarnation are destroyed by the Holy Throne of Shuizhi, the three women and the three reincarnations will face a very dangerous situation!

"..."

The evil water column that concealed the sky and the sun ignored Xiao Yan's attack and headed towards the Tomb of Reincarnation Light, but when it was about to attack the latter, there was a strange surging around the Tomb of Reincarnation, the void trembled, and the latter attacked Drown out of thin air!

The three tomb symbiosis method naturally did not hinder Xiao Yan and the others who were carrying Samsara and Saint Flame, but they were very hostile to evil forces. That kind of mysterious defensive power was not something that any cat or dog could destroy.

"Holy Seat, the Xiao Yan in front of him is the leader of the Endless Flame Alliance. Now, with the strength of our Shuizhi Saint Clan, it may be difficult to capture him... In order to be safe, let's call the other two adults as soon as possible."

"Xiao Yan's repeated killings of powerful people from outside the territory have already made the top leaders outside the territory quite dissatisfied. Don't miss the opportunity for the sake of making meritorious deeds!"

Looking at such a bizarre scene, in the dark passage behind the Tianhe, there is a sound of evil.

If there are so many sources of holy flames, if they are fully integrated and displayed by Xiao Yan, even the Shuizhi Holy Throne will be difficult to deal with!

"It's nonsense, and the eight-fold reincarnation hierarchy can still obliterate this seat?"

"Thousands of demons obey the order and seal the space. When this seat captures them, the leader of the outside world will definitely reward our clan. At that time, the two guys at the head of the holy clan will naturally look at their own clan with admiration. !"

In the face of the latter evil shadow's persuasion, the Shuizhi Holy Throne disapproved, and looked at Xiao Yan with a grim expression, with an exceptionally powerful aura.

Void, mountains, mountain peaks, pieces of dark and outrageous evil aura, gushing out continuously, completely shrouded the whole world!

In this dark night, Xiao Yan's body and the three reincarnation tombs were extremely dazzling.

"Just to my liking."

Seeing that the other party was so arrogant, Xiao Yan also slightly relieved.

The Holy Throne of Shuizhi has this idea, and Xiao Yan himself also has this idea!

Since breaking through to the Eighth Reincarnation Rank, he has not fought well, and now this Water Childhood Holy Throne will undoubtedly become his best trial stone at present!

The night fell, the space was sealed, and the holy flame in Xiao Yan's body was like a provocative holy beast, gradually becoming violent, and the holy breath gradually became stalemate against it.

"It's raining?"

Xiao Yan looked up, but found that in this strange void, there were suddenly countless strange black spots, and the black spots contained a puzzling destructive power!

Xiao Yan stared intently at the black dots in the dark night, and found that they were actually slowly connecting into a jet-black ray, crowding out neatly and orderly in this world.

Seeing such a strange movement in front of him, Xiao Yan suddenly realized something.

The black dots, rays, keep getting bigger and bigger, and after just an instant, a huge net of black rays and black dots appeared in the whole world.

"Xiao Yan, this is the death gift this seat specially prepared for you."

Seeing Xiao Yan's body stagnant in the void, the gloomy smile of the Shuizhi Holy Throne spread to every corner of this world!

"Is it a fighting skill after the fusion of water attributes and evil forces between demigods and quasi-divine arts?"

Looking at the sky-shaking water curtain and water net formed by more and more evil black spots and death rays around him, the eight sources of holy flames in Xiao Yan's hands became quite frantic, and waves of heat waved from them. It poured out and slapped Xiao Yan's body continuously.

"If that's the case... then try the fusion of the seventeen holy flames."

After hesitating for a moment, feeling the hatred of many holy flames for evil forces, Xiao Yan couldn't help but have a really crazy idea in his heart.

The seventeen holy flames occupy almost half of the holy flame list, and the combined fighting skills are powerful enough to compete with the Shuizhi Holy Throne... I just don't know if such a fusion can reach the level of quasi-god fighting skills. category?

After that, the eight-color holy flames above Xiao Yan's hands began to surge, rising like a scorching sun, and the nine-color holy flames in his body were also called and climbed out together.

Seventeen brilliant flame halos surrounded Xiao Yan's body slightly, making this guy look like an ancient emperor who was deeply embraced by flames!

"This is... the fusion of holy flames?"

"Holy Seat, you have to think twice!"

Seeing Xiao Yan's crazy fusion move, many of the confidants of the Shuizhi saints in the passageway looked gloomy, and even some fears spread out.

In the Endless Fire Territory, Xiao Yan, the fusion of holy flames has long been one of his trump cards... Now the number of such fusion holy flames has reached an unprecedented seventeen.

"Hurry up, what's your style?"

"This seat definitely doesn't believe that he can integrate such a variety of holy flames."

"Water Curtain... Rotten Heaven Realm!"

In an instant, the Shuizhi Holy Throne did not hesitate, stepping heavily on the evil Tianhe, and a voice resounding through the heavens and the earth swept out.

Obviously at this time, there is a trace of unease in his heart... But he underestimated Xiao Yan's ability Now he is getting the Flame God Cauldron, not to mention the fusion of the seventeen holy flames, even if it is the fusion of all holy Yan, for Xiao Yan, is also an easy matter.

Above the evil Tianhe in the sky, a huge gap suddenly opened, and countless evil forces seemed to have found a sloping opening in an instant, and shot towards Xiao Yan below!

The endless evil forces formed an evil waterfall that shook the sky, countless black spots and rays formed a giant net of death in the sky, three completely different forces, completely surrounded Xiao Yan, and could launch a fatal blow against the latter at any time.

"Give me fusion!"

Even at this time of crisis, Xiao Yan's lips suddenly showed a proud and fearless smile. With the operation of the burning art in his body, the seventeen holy flames began to merge under the sight of countless powerhouses.

As for the devastating attack of the Shuizhi Holy Seat, Xiao Yan didn't seem to plan to defend, but had other plans instead!
