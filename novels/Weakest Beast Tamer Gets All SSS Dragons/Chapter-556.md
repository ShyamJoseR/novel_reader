Chapter 556: Chapter 556 – Taming the Fourth Year: The Bigger Picture

“Yes, also exactly that color,” Zhao answered with a tense voice. “I saw it clearly during that expedition.”

Sirius made a sound of frustration before addressing Selphira.

“I have to go,” he told her urgently. “Those creatures are already deviating down there, and the projections are useless. When they begin to search more broadly and erratically, they’ll soon be invading the surface. And not just Yino’s civilians are going to pay the consequences of a passive approach on our part!”

“You made a promise,” Selphira responded with a firm but tired voice. “If you do this, you’re going to lose the opportunity to reclaim the clan you’ve waited so long to rebuild.”

Sirius shouted at her, his voice charged with accumulated frustration:

“What opportunity?! The chamber opened and although Luna was there, the situation didn’t change as we expected. Being so young, she can’t claim her part yet because we didn’t end up acting according to protocol. And with things as they are, taking any crystals out would be an invitation to attack from a complete united front of those idiots. One that, without Dragarion, we could only repel by betting tens of thousands of innocent casualties.”

His voice became more bitter.

“The clan’s recovery, the supreme leader’s seat… Luna must take it anyway. Half the promise doesn’t make sense now with the door open so soon. So I could go and do something of real benefit.”

“Things shouldn’t stay like this,” Selphira insisted. “Wait for counsel from Julius and Arturo too. If you leave the seal’s range, they’re going to open it and use it against you when you return. The Gold 3 ring is too far to come back in time.”

“Selphira, you’re not going to do it!” Sirius finally complained. “The three princes won’t either. Nobody has the necessary requirements, and if I don’t do it, we don’t know if we’ll be able to survive an assault.”

“We can withstand a few years without problem,” Selphira argued.

“Not if there’s internal fighting!” Sirius replied. “There’s no point waiting, defending passively, if there’s no longer new hope! The boy lost our best powers and is no longer the light of hope you believed. I told you from the beginning. All that’s left is for me to go while the idiots take over my star but help you contain the beasts. And when I return, hopefully with another part of the family treasure, then I can tear it from their dirty hands.”

“It will already be ‘profaned’ even if you manage to recover it,” Selphira murmured with pain. “And I know you’ll never get over that.”

“We’ll both live with it,” Sirius responded with a broken voice, “here and in heaven, as long as Luna can reach the final goal.”

Ren felt as if he had been struck. The implications of what he was hearing were enormous. There were references to a clan, to a family treasure, to requirements and protocols he didn’t completely understand. But what he did understand better now was the pain in Sirius’s voice and a bit of the situation that had been hidden behind Luna’s tears.

Her family was at the center of some complex political situation, something that involved not only Luna’s clan but now also the kingdom’s defenses against the underground creatures that had been discovered.

And apparently, he had been considered part of some solution until he lost that part of his power.

The weight of this realization also hit him. It wasn’t just that his loss of abilities affected him personally; there were political and military consequences he hadn’t considered.

Had Luna lost some kind of hope with him too?

She was trapped in the middle of all this, probably feeling responsible for situations that were even more beyond her control.

Suddenly, her strange behavior, her anger toward her father, and even her declaration of “enmity” toward him took on a completely different meaning.

It was about the impossible pressure her family was facing and the way his loss of power had changed political equations he didn’t even know existed.

♢♢♢♢

While continuing to listen to the conversation, Ren caught a specific location that made everything begin to make sense.

In the Gold 3 ring…

Ren started thinking as he silently moved away from the place. With the information he had from before losing his fungus, combined with what Zhao had told him after his biggest expedition, he began to piece together the puzzle.

The “tree that looked like a claw” that Zhao had mentioned in his reports, similar to the one that had marked the tunnel toward his fungus’s improvement years ago.

Maybe what Sirius was looking for was some artifact or medicine that could help improve the general situation. Something that could give them an advantage against the mutant creatures that were becoming an increasingly greater threat.

Ren wanted to think that all the knowledge he had shared had left the city better prepared, but when imagining a relentless assault from mutant beasts, he realized the limitations.

Having many more Silver tamers was definitely a good thing, but it didn’t feel sufficient for a proactive attack. Only for defending. And Silver tamers who were still in formation, if they were sent underground in combat expeditions, many would die in battle.

But there was an even bigger problem. If the mutants were absorbing all the small beasts to create their clones, the quantity would be ridiculous. And they would never stop increasing their numbers even when attacked relentlessly if the capture range of new small beasts was very wide.

It was like facing an infinite enemy that dawned equal or stronger after each defeat inflicted on them, growing more with each battle.

Traditional warfare assumed finite resources and attrition working both ways. But an enemy that grew stronger violated those fundamental assumptions, making conventional military strategy obsolete.

He analyzed his current capabilities while walking back toward the dormitories. His Silver 2 hydra and Silver 1 wolverine were still powerful, but without his fungus’s bonuses and the rings, he was operating at approximately half his previous capacity.

In his previous expedition to the depths, he had fought against Gold 3 creatures and won with considerable difficulty. Gold 3 with half his power would represent a significant jump in danger.

Additionally, there was the question of the journey itself.

The distance to the Gold 3 ring represented weeks of travel through increasingly dangerous territory. Without his enhanced perception and analytical abilities, navigation alone would be challenging, let alone the combat encounters he would inevitably face.

So the question that tormented him was simple but crucial: could he reach the Gold 3 ring and get them what they wanted in his present situation?
