# Chapter Vol 4 2729: Shuizhi, the 1st of the 3 saints!

"The method of placing this kind of cemetery has long been lost. The three links are intertwined and coexist together. It is somewhat similar to the self-protection array of your Xiao family in ancient times."

Looking at the three tombs of light, although the identity of the reincarnation powerhouse sleeping in it has been determined, Su Xueying shook her head slightly. Obviously, this kind of thing also has a lot of background, which made her a little jealous.

"What magic circle?"

Su Xueying used to be in the line of the guardians of the Xiao family, and now she has won many inheritances in the demon world. It is not difficult to know these things, but Xiao Yan and a few people around her don't know much about it. After all, this magic formation is too obscure and difficult to understand.

"Three Tombs Symbiosis Method."

"This is a very strange formation... Once it is cast, the life of the caster and the binder will be completely bound together within a certain period of time."

"All glory to all."

"Combined with the existence of the two black holes just outside the cemetery hall, I am afraid this is Elder Xiao Yan's means to protect those two adults."

Su Xueying was restless, and said word by word what she had guessed.

"What is the mystery in this magic circle?"

Xun'er and Mu Yingying glanced at each other, knowing little about this magic circle that existed in ancient times.

"Although the magic circle does not have too many terrifying powers, it is an excellent shelter for the three tombs of light from poisonous hands."

"There have been countless fierce battles here. I am afraid that if the Great Elder hadn't cast the magic circle, the reincarnation of the other two would have been broken."

Su Xueying spoke up again, dragging out all the thoughts that lingered in her mind.

"Is the magic circle dangerous?"

Xiao Yan's huge soul power had no effect for a while, so he could only turn around and ask Su Xueying.

"The three of us have the Reincarnation Seal, and we have the existence of sacred fire. For us, there is no danger."

Su Xueying said firmly, but the latter did not tell the danger in reincarnation.

"Since there is no danger in it, start preparing."

"The time required for the reincarnation is still unknown."

"After you enter the reincarnation cemetery, I will protect you here day and night."

Xiao Yan opened his mouth, and his voice was also full of seriousness.

The first elder Xiao Yan, the Xiao clan goddess of war Xiao Lingshan, and the love Lang Jiuxuan Huozhu of the Phoenix clan's patriarch, these three played a very important role for them.

"Yep."

The three women nodded at the same time, trying their best to move the reincarnation mark between the eyebrows, and poured in towards the three reincarnation light tombs little by little.

The three Xeons have been trapped in reincarnation for countless times. If they want to forcibly awaken them, they must be guided by the reincarnation seal and enter it in person.

If successful, these Xeons will wake up again, and the three women will also gain the true power of reincarnation, but if they fail, I am afraid that everyone will be drowned in the long river of history, which can be described as dangerous.

"Susu."

"Everyone, why are you so anxious?"

Just as the three girls approached the Tomb of Reincarnation, the surrounding void twisted strangely. The three tombs of light and countless mountain peaks trembled, as if they had encountered a terrifying force that shocked them.

"Boom!"

The deafening magical sound of heaven and earth swept out from the hall, turning into a turbulent flow of time and space, shaking this mountain range and the peaks of the mountain with a humming sound.

The extremely evil magic power turned into a dark passage in the high altitude of the mountain range, and there were countless evil auras in the passage... At the end of these auras, there was a burly figure, slowly walking out of it.

When his human-like body completely appeared in front of Xiao Yan and the others, the sticky magic power slanted down from his body like a tide of energy, and many peaks in the mountains were easily crushed, squeezed, and melted later. For all the powder.

"Saint powerhouse?"

Looking at the group of evil black shadows in the dark passage, which were as large as ants, the corners of Xiao Yan's eyes twitched violently when he felt the soul-shaking supreme evil power above the burly figure in front of him!

This is the most difficult person from outside the realm that he has encountered since he entered the Eighth Reincarnation Rank!

"Xiao Yan's Reincarnation Tomb... It really makes this seat easy to find!"

The Shuizhi Holy Throne's eyes are deep, with endless murderous intentions inside. He glanced at this world at will, and noticed the abnormality above the three groups of Samsara Light Tombs.

There is some kind of indecipherable connection between the three groups of Reincarnation Light Tombs, and even the faint overflowing fluctuations, even he felt a chill!

But so what?

Now that they have fallen into a deep sleep, how can they be their opponents?

"You... and retreat, this place has been occupied by me."

"Or do you want to be my enemy?"

The Shuizhi Holy Throne Phantom stared at Xiao Yan with emotionless eyes, and immediately opened his mouth full of resentment.

A mere eight-layer reincarnation rank, and he has to protect the three girls around him, how to fight with himself and the evil soldiers behind him?

"Pfft!"

When the sight of the shadow of the Shuizhi Holy Throne locked on Xiao Yan and the others, except for Xiao Yan, a huge pressure suddenly appeared on the delicate bodies of the other three girls, which instantly made them tremble slightly, and they would completely kneel at any time. go down!

How can their current strength resist the pressure of the Water Childhood Holy Throne at the pinnacle of the Eighth Reincarnation Rank?

"You go first... I'll deal with it here."

Xiao Yan watched the opponent's terrifying offensive, his teeth clenched, and the holy flame within his body began to rise little by little. There was no trace of fear in his dark pupils, and he directly faced the latter!

"But……"

Seeing this catastrophe, Mu Yingying couldn't bear it in her heart.

"If you don't go in, you won't have time!"

The next moment, Xiao Yan almost roared.

A mere Saint Clan powerhouse, although it doubled the pressure on him, but it was not an invincible situation. If the three girls chose to help him at this time, once the other two Saint Clan powerhouses arrived here, let alone Xiao Yan himself would die. Unknown, even the three tombs of reincarnation in front of them are probably in danger!

"Yingying!"

Just when Mu Yingying was about to speak again, Xun'er spoke sternly and directly stopped the latter.

Nowadays, the situation has not changed much. Once you take a wrong step, everyone will be trapped in a state of doom!

"This is the source of holy flame fire that should be able to help you."

"No... ugh."

Xiao Yan wanted to refuse, but seeing Xun'er's strong appearance, he swallowed the words hard.

Seeing Xun'er's serious expression, the other two women also instantly understood the powerful relationship and followed suit.

"Go... I believe in you."

"Just leave it to me here."

It was not until the ancient imprint of reincarnation flashed on the body of the three girls again that Xiao Yan leaked a confident smile to the three girls.

"go!"

The three women looked at each other, and with the power of the Reincarnation Seal, they turned into a wave of light and poured directly into the Reincarnation Cemetery!

"Want to go?"

"I have to ask this seat too, right?"

The Shuizhi Holy Throne seemed to have expected it long ago. Looking at the hurried figures of the three women, when they waved their hands, a pitch-black river of thousands of meters was all over their body.

In the river, there are endless scarlet dazzling blood lights, such a Tianhe, I am afraid that the blood of thousands of human beings has been integrated into it. Once waved, the evil power generated may completely shock this world!
