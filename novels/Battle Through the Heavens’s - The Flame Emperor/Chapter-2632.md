# Chapter Vol 4 2580: 9 Tail Demon Palace!

"What a strange demon temple..."

At night, Xiao Yan and the others came to the Nine-Tailed Demon Palace under the leadership of Nine-Tailed Qingluan.

Standing in front of the demon temple, an invisible coercion of the soul spread out from it. In addition to the slightly illusory nine-tailed demon shadow energy body above the main entrance of the demon temple, Xiao Yan couldn't help but produce a surge of energy. Weird touch.

His soul power has already reached the Immortal Ascension Boundary, filling up the eighth-layer spiritual palace. With such a powerful soul power, does he feel a little bit strange?

In the temple of the nine-tailed demon fox, are there all kinds of terrifying powers that shake the world?

Or maybe there is a strong person who also belongs to the soul power of the Immortal Ascension Boundary level, which makes you feel like this?

"Before entering it, there is one more strange thing to tell you."

After pondering for a while, Jiuwei Qingluan's eyes moved, and she was no longer hiding anything. After all, their entire race was helpless when it came to domination, and now they could only pin their hopes on Xiao Yan and the three of them.

"All ears."

Xiao Yan's expression was very calm, but he couldn't help but feel nervous for Cailin.

"A long time ago, the patriarch of the Nine-Tails Clan once broke into the demon temple alone, but there was no trace after that. Even if we exhausted countless methods, we couldn't find any trace of her."

"According to some of our obscure investigations, there seems to be some aura that does not belong to our nine-tailed clan in the demon hall. As for the specific aura, we can't help but know."

Nine-tailed Qingluan told the important information of the nine-tailed clan, and naturally hoped that Xiao Yan would take back the tyrannical weapon.

"Doesn't it belong to the nine-tailed clan..."

Xiao Yan couldn't help but feel a little surprised. Combining his own guesses about evil aura, he seemed to have a premonition, but he didn't say much.

If these things are true, then it is good to solve them by yourself.

As soon as the voice fell, Jiuwei Qingluan's jade hand twitched, and there was a dazzling light on his body, which shot directly at the main entrance of the Nine-Tailed Demon Palace!

"Shhhhhh!"

The nine-tailed demon shadow perched on it gave a contempt, a cyan halo burst out, and the ancient and powerful nine-tailed shadow trembled, instantly shrouding Xiao Yan and the three of them, and included them in it.

"I hope this time, it will be successful."

Looking at this scene, Jiuwei Qingluan's eyes were filled with hope.

"Boom!"

In an instant, just when Xiao Yan and the three of them had just rushed into the Demon Palace, there were several samsara auras in the quarters of the Nine-Tails clan, which quickly gathered towards the Demon Palace.

"Great Elder!"

One after another, low-pitched roars carrying monstrous anger resounded.

"I have made up my mind. This time, the matter of the patriarch and the tyrant must be completely resolved. This matter does not need to be discussed..."

In the face of the obstruction of these stubborn elements in the clan, Jiuwei Qingluan snorted coldly. Behind her, there was also a small nine-tailed fox dormant.

"Xiao Yan... did you hear that sound?"

The three of them entered the demon hall, and first they passed through a long dark passage, and walked aimlessly in it, but Cai Lin's brows were tightly wrinkled, and she looked around with some vigilance.

Even the power of the ten-colored sky-swallowing python that was quietly flowing in his skin released a strange wave... as if there was an aura that made him feel disgusted!

"what sound?"

Xiao Yan heard the words and came back, but he didn't hear any sound.

"I didn't hear it either."

Die Qi looked very focused at this time.

"Baby... the sound of a baby crying!"

Cai Lin spoke solemnly, this voice seemed to touch the power in her body, but no matter what Cai Lin said, Xiao Yan and Xiao Yan didn't seem to hear the same thing at all.

"Eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee!"

The immature baby's voice came out from this space again, and it fell crisply in Cailin's ears.

Suddenly, an angelica-colored light flickered, illuminating all the void in front of him, and the original dark passage suddenly became clear and opened up.

This is a land of ruins. There are countless large and small prismatic mirrors around the ruins. These mirrors are incomplete, and even the placement is quite messy, but the silhouettes of Xiao Yan and the three are reflected on them.

"In the center, there is an altar."

"The baby's voice came from there."

Xiao Yan and Cai Lin glanced at each other, but they both noticed each other, only Die Qi, who looked like a superfluous person, was left.

Walking quietly, it seems that this void is like a dead place, without any surging energy fluctuations, like a place where the energy of heaven and earth is deficient.

"Boom!"

Just as the three of them watched their surroundings vigilantly and walked towards the mirror-faced central altar, the holy flame that had been quiet in Xiao Yan's body burst out with a sense of disgust.

The source of this fluctuation of holy flame is only the evil aura of the evil race from outside the territory!

But in this space, there is no trace of energy. What is going on?

After a while, they came to the edge of the altar.

This altar, the whole body is snow-white, is made up of countless snow-white mirrors piled up, forming a circular altar about a hundred feet long.

Looking around, in the center of the altar, there is a silver stone statue as tall and short as a human being, standing quietly in it.

This silver stone statue has a snow-white light lingering all over its body, and the figure is the appearance of a graceful and unparalleled human woman.

Even if it was a stone statue, the moment his eyes fixed on the latter, Xiao Yan was able to discover the beauty of this stone statue woman, how endearing it was, but behind her, there were nine stones carved from exquisite stones. Tail.

In the altar, there is only the stone statue of the woman and the nine tails behind her, and there is nothing else.

"What a terrifying demon."

Cai Lin narrowed her eyes slightly, but the power of the Ten-Colored Sky Swallowing Python in her body was quiet, as if the object in front of her could not evoke its resonance!

Either this stone statue is just a blind eye, or the power left by the nine-tailed demon fox has dissipated.

When Cailin and Dieqi were doing nothing Xiao Yan's eyes never left the stone statue.

"This stone statue is weird!"

"retreat!"

But just as Xiao Yan's voice fell, the stone statue suddenly trembled, and a burst of pure white light wafted out of it.

The light flickered, and after a while, it turned into a baby the size of a few palms.

The baby was like a newborn baby who fell to the ground. He opened his clear eyes and stared at the two in front of him. After a while, he babbled and spoke in the direction of Cailin: "Mother...Mother...sir"

"..."

Xiao Yan did not respond, but kept his eyes on the stone statue and this strange baby.

When Cai Lin was called out like this, her eyes suddenly became scattered, and she opened her hands in a daze, as if she wanted to hug this little guy in her arms!

"Shhhhhh!"

Looking at the scene of Cailin, the baby actually showed a jerky smile and flashed away at Cailin!

At the moment when it flickered, a thick **** smell spread from its body, and the intensity of the **** fluctuation turned out to make this altar mirror world slightly illusory!
