# Chapter Vol 4 2767: The beginning of the great melee

"boom!"

Immediately, with the power of qi and blood pouring into both the Baxia Dragon King and the Fire Dragon King, everyone could only vaguely see that the whole world of the Dragon Tomb was suddenly shrouded in a golden light curtain, and there seemed to be a certain figure in it. struggling.

The golden light was extremely powerful, and even if the two dragon kings tried to neutralize them with the power of qi and blood, the effect was minimal.

Along with this irresistible golden light and shadow Dragon King, there was even a hoarse, heart-piercing scream that filled the air.

The kind of blood of the Golden Dragon King, which is enough to crush any human being, is so troublesome to absorb!

For a time, the expressions of the Dragon King and the others suddenly froze, even if everything they did was trivial, they were still sending Xiao Yan the power of qi and blood.

With such a cowardly body to forcibly absorb the power of the Golden Dragon King's blood essence, the torment and pain that he needs to endure far exceeds that of the devil's body.

"..."

Until Xiao Yan took the initiative to release the blood essence power of the Golden Dragon King, the whole world was violent, and countless old dragon roars were transmitted from his body, echoing in this void!

That kind of terrifying fluctuations that make people feel dizzy, so that no one in the dragon family can resist!

"Fight!"

Xiao Yan felt the extremely vast power contained in this golden blood, and immediately gritted his teeth, countless power surged together in his body, and directly resisted it.

If he can't resist the erosion of the golden power, the end will be nothing more than annihilation of body and spirit... But if he survives it, I am afraid that the reward will make Xiao Yan truly touch the peak of this great world!

In the deep eyes, the colors of madness and perseverance are constantly alternating, the pain of the bone peeling and skinning makes the demon power and the power of the Xiao clan in the body tremble slightly, but Xiao Yan does not show any signs of slackening. The golden liquid found any flaws, and all that was waiting for Xiao Yan was the end of ashes.

Time flies little by little, no one knows what kind of horrific torture Xiao Yan has experienced, only countless heart-piercing screams swept out from the sky and the earth.

However, as the screams became smaller and smaller, the golden liquid that was enough to destroy Xiao Yan was like a wild horse being tamed. The special pattern, the surging power of the golden pattern, seems to have an ancient wave that surpasses the heavens and the earth, constantly nourishing, repairing the latter's dying body!

"impossible……"

At the moment when Xiao Yan's whole body was shaking like this, Long Feng was not idle either. Countless powers were exerted. The keel and legs surged together and shot directly at Xiao Yan. Such terrifying power, the latter possessed Enough self-confidence to completely obliterate Xiao Yan, but his incomparably confident attack was like a grain of sand, unable to disturb Xiao Yan's figure, and was engulfed by the golden halo.

Especially Xiao Yan's aura of the overlord of the dragon clan made Long Feng's complexion so moved that there were many traces of looseness in his body's strength!

That is the power of the Golden Dragon King!

A class of human beings actually possessed the blood of the Golden Dragon King, and desperately devoured them in front of everyone's eyes?

"Guru..."

All the attacks were annihilated like moths to the fire, even the keel on the legs trembled slightly, fearing the golden figure above the sky.

Long Feng's eyes flickered slightly, and when he saw Xiao Yan with pale golden light shining all over his body, the power of reincarnation in his body, even the keel, produced a very humble emotion.

"How can you possibly have the power of the Golden Dragon King?"

"And with the mere eight-fold reincarnation rank peak strength, it has completely absorbed it?"

"impossible!"

"This is definitely a fantasy!"

Looking at the golden figure moving slightly like a **** of death, overlapping time and space, Long Feng's body trembled and he spoke nonsense.

Countless powerhouses saw a golden light and shadow slide across the sky, and then the rapidly expanding legs of Long Feng and the Tianyu keel in the mid-air seemed to be wrapped by some kind of golden halo power that no one could understand. During any hesitation, the crisp sound of broken bones resounded.

At that moment, it seemed like something was broken.

"Crack!"

The swollen legs exploded instantly, blood overflowing, and even the keel, which was regarded as the supreme sacred object by the dragon race, first had a crisp sound of shattering, and then it turned into powder in the sky under the gaze of everyone.

Such a move shocked all the powerhouses present.

"Broken... Broken?"

"The keel is broken?"

"Crush the Dragon Bone with your bare hands?"

Among the dragon powerhouses in the battle between the two sides, I don't know who said it, and immediately looked at the yellow powder entrenched in the sky in a stunned manner, and the shock in their hearts could not calm down for a long time.

It seems that this time, they have provoked some people who can't be provoked.

"Hoohoo."

It wasn't until this moment that Yun Yun and Cai Lin patted their chests and let out a heavy breath... Fortunately, there was no danger.

And at the moment when Long Feng was defeated, a terrifying energy ripple surged above the magnificent gate at the entrance of the Dragon Tomb, slowly opening.

"The entrance to the Dragon Tomb is broken!"

"Go, enter the dragon tomb!"

As soon as the words fell, the Baxia Dragon King and the Fire Dragon King stood up first, and took the vast army behind them and swept away together!

"Xiao Yan... and Long Feng's breath disappeared at the same time, where did they go?"

Seeing that the allied army of Baxia Longwang and others took the lead in taking action, the four dragon kings with dull eyes looked at each other, and the shock in their hearts could not be calmed down for a long time.

The dignified dragon clan's supreme elder, Long Feng, with half-step nine-fold reincarnation level strength, plus the power of Tianyu keel, was so defeated!

After swallowing the blood of the Golden Dragon King, Xiao Yan's combat power has really reached such an appalling level?

"What should we do now...?"

Watching the Baxia Dragon King's coalition forces quickly swept towards the entrance of the dragon tomb, the Tulong King said with a sullen face, and there was also a burst of grains in his heart.

The elder Taishang was defeated, and they could no longer stop the other party from entering the dragon tomb. Could it be that they could only sit still and wait for the trial of the Golden Dragon King?

"The Earth Dragon King Relying on you alone, intercepting the allied forces of Baxia and others with Earth-based demigods, they must not be allowed to step into the dragon tomb."

"I'll wait for three people, enter the dragon tomb, and stop them!"

"If we don't fight back at this time, once the matter of the Silver Dragon King is revealed, our fate will be irreversible!"

The dark dragon king's eyes flashed with unprecedented solemnity. Before, they had the support of the elders, and they could fish in troubled waters. Now they are the only ones left.

As soon as the voice fell, everyone also acted separately.

So far, the powerhouses who have entered the Dragon Tomb are the Yin Dragon King faction Storm Dragon King, Dark Dragon King, Star Dragon King, Cailin, Yunyun, Baxia Dragon King, Fire Dragon King and others, and the rest, all of whom are covered by the Earth Dragon King. One person intercepted him in the dragon tomb with a huge fighting skill of the earth attribute... As for the wooden dragon king, he was still unable to break free from Xiao Yan's shackles, and he was also unable to intervene in this civil strife.
