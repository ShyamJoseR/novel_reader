# Chapter Vol 4 2587: Mysterious cave, where the monsters are!

"It seems... this is the so-called Four Elephant Cave, but this world is somewhat inconsistent with the second strange place in countless legends in the demon world."

"Snake monster?"

"This is... the three evil spirit snakes?"

Due to the dim light, you can vaguely see the three-headed monster snakes dormant in the black clouds. The snake scales around the body are covered with the sky-high monster energy, and three huge snake heads, each with dozens of The size of the three pairs of scarlet snake eyes is like six lanterns, moving in the gloomy void, bursting with blood-red monsters, which is extremely terrifying.

"Tear."

The three-headed monsters seemed to be under some kind of threat. The huge snake head shook, swallowing the snake letter, and gusts of gloomy wind suddenly swept out from the three giant mouths, carrying a gloomy poison, eroding everything around.

"It seems that this guy is also a creature in the Four Elephants Cave, and the brief fight just now convinced him."

"Let's go, let's go and have a look."

The two looked at each other, their speed skyrocketed, and they chased away directly in the direction in which the three monsters fled.

"Susu... Is someone finally here?"

At this moment, in the depths of the dark void, there was an ancient and turbid voice that quietly spoke.

"This is?"

When the three-headed monster moved to the entrance of a dark cave, it suddenly stopped, the whole body quietly escaped from the air, and lay quietly on the ground, with a look of respect in the snake's eyes.

"This attack was sent by the three monsters... It seems to make us withdraw, and there are some terrifying creatures hidden inside."

The huge sound shook the entire cave, and the momentum was astonishing. Cailin seemed to sense some thoughts in the latter's heart, and couldn't help but say.

"The monster we were looking for should exist here."

"I have observed along the way. This is the depth of the entire Four Elephants Cave. If expected, this is the core."

Although the cave is narrow, it is bottomless. The two walked hand in hand to the depths of the cave, but the further down the cave, the more dignified the gloomy atmosphere became. Feel a touch of unease.

It seems that at the bottom of the cave, there are some peerless beasts waiting for them.

"Devouring!"

During the movement along the way, Xiao Yan released the fire of holy flame slightly to illuminate the path forward, and then a dark swallowing power filled his body. He did not dare to approach Xiao Yan at all.

After all, that devouring power is enough to devour everything, and the weak creatures in the cave are terrified and can only retreat.

Walking like this, it was close to a quarter of an hour, and the line of sight in front of the two became wider. It seemed that they had reached the end of the cave, and a whole new world poured into their eyes.

At the end of the cave, there seemed to be a shattered prison, but at this time there was no coercion, and Xiao Yan and the two easily crossed.

The world in front of him was a scene of a hundred-zhang-sized cave area, with a fist-sized luminous pearl floating above it in the center, illuminating the entire world.

Xiao Yan did not know whether it was the attraction force of the divine weapon, or other fluctuations, but Xiao Yan vaguely knew that this cave was definitely a fierce place!

"Here... there seems to be the aura of that monster, although it's very weak."

After a while, Cailin's beautiful eyes were slowly staring into the sarcophagus. Judging from the power of the ten colors, Cailin seemed to smell some hidden aura of the monster from the cave.

"Human... save me."

"The countless treasures in these four elephant caves are all for you."
