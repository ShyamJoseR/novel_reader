Yaoji Soul Shadow slowly turned her head and looked at the strange crystal layers above the sky. After confirming it again and again, she was relieved.

Could this be Yao Ji's true strength?

When Yao Ji was swallowed up by herself, was it premeditated?

What's the secret behind this?

"The evil corpse god, just capture it."

After a simple fight, the evil corpse **** was completely at a disadvantage, and the figures of Xiao Yan and the three instantly appeared beside the latter and surrounded him!

The terrifying existence that ranks second among the foreign evil races, if they can be captured alive, I am afraid that they will get a lot of useful information for the Great Thousand World!

"Even if this seat is smashed to pieces, it will never make you feel better!"

The weak figure in the black fog shook for a while, and there was an abnormal expansion, and the sound of tearing and whistling poured out.
