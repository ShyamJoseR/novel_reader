# Chapter Vol 4 2764: Tianyu Keel (Part 1)

"I'm not ashamed...but I won't give you this chance!"

Facing Xiao Yan's extremely arrogant tone, a playful smile flashed across the eyes of the Supreme Elder Long Feng.

The reason why he is willing to be loyal to the Silver Dragon King is naturally that he has obtained enough huge benefits from the latter's hands. In front of that thing, the power of the Silver Dragon King is just a sweetness, and the real benefit lies in his own legs.

The thing that gathered the legs and the breath of the Silver Dragon King combined attack, and with all his strength, even facing the real quasi-ninth-level reincarnation-level powerhouse is enough to compete, and now such a cowardly Xiao Yan, take the What to crack it?

Although his heart is full of contempt, Long Feng is a cautious person, and naturally he will not take risks. With a slight force in his legs, his handprint changes rapidly, and the energy dragon that controls Xiao Yan roars out.

The dragon's breath, the dragon's flames, and the dragon's claws were constantly alternated, causing Xiao Yan to retreat one after another.

What's more peculiar, Long Feng's legs have a faint crystalline silver color, and the inside of the entire right leg is as beautiful as a gorgeous crystal inlaid in it!

Looking at Xiao Yan's bizarre tricks, Long Feng's eyes closed slightly, his handprints changed rapidly again, and the dim light flickering from his thick legs was about to escape like a swirl of a bow.

"The way of hell, the way of beasts... the power of the six ways, stop it for me!"

Xiao Yan's eyes have been watching the strange power of the latter's legs, and he can only act like this as a last resort.

"Hmph, how can the power of the ants be compared with the powerhouses of the dragon race like me?"

"With remorse, go to hell."

Looking at Xiao Yan's unmoved figure, Long Feng raised his legs slightly, turned sideways, the surrounding space was completely rippling at this moment, and an extremely dangerous aura slowly permeated.

In the originally noisy battlefield, it gradually became quiet, and one after another panicked eyes raised at the same time, looking at the entrance of the Dragon Tomb... Where is the place with the most dangerous fluctuations.

"What an annoying bunch of flies."

At this moment, Yun Yun's originally indifferent eyes also contained a rare trace of anger. The powerful attacks of herself and the two dragon kings were completely resisted by the other four dragon kings one after another, and Xiao Yan's situation became more and more dangerous.

"Tear!"

Since the end of the Sea of ​​Samsara, under the guidance of the Great Elder, Xiao Yan has not only won the mysterious fire attribute quasi-divine fighting skills, but even the origin skills of the third holy flame, the six-path psychic, and the hidden quasi-sacred skills. Divine art, six flames destroying the world, mastered together, the combat effectiveness can be said to have a qualitative leap,

"Huhu...it should be almost there."

In an instant, Xiao Yan twisted his body, his quiet eyes glanced at the world at will, and finally locked on Long Feng who was stepping through the void and kicking at him.

"call."

He let out a sigh of relief, and Xiao Yan's whole body was suddenly filled with extremely powerful fluctuations. The blue-colored dragon scales, and even a faint golden ripple was tilted down from his body.

The ancient power of the Dragon God Transformation and the absolute coercion over the dragon race finally unfolded at this moment.

"Gulu...Dragon God Transformation?"

"You are an outsider, and you have actually cultivated the supreme law of my dragon clan... It seems that you are certain to die today."

Until this moment, the fear and anxiety in Long Feng's heart expanded rapidly, and the speed was even faster. In a blink of an eye, he broke through the entanglement of the six energy puppets and the energy dragon, and shot at Xiao Yan's vital point.

Even if the latter cultivates with the Dragon God Transformation, he will definitely not be able to resist such a hard blow that combines the breath of the Silver Dragon King and the strength of his legs.

The strong energy fluctuations turned into countless ferocious energy, which swept towards Xiao Yan's body without reservation. Even with the protection of the Dragon Scale, Xiao Yan's body was still flickering with trembling fluctuations.

With the power of the Dragon Scale alone, it is impossible to defend against the devastating blow of Long Feng.

At the same time, Xiao Yan's hands simultaneously gathered a very majestic energy.

Above the left hand, a dazzling golden halo quickly flowed out from the limbs and bones in the body, and vaguely gathered into a claw shape. In the depths of the soul, there is a sense of adoration.

On the other hand, five fingers stretched out, and a trace of obscure gray lightning arcs lingered on his fingertips like a gray liquid. The moment it surged, the world changed color.
