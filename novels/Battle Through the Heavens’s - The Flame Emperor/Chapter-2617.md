# Chapter Vol 4 2565: Hell, havoc!

Xiao Yan didn't know how long he had been advancing. When Xiao Yan opened his eyes again, the environment in front of him and around him had already undergone earth-shaking changes.

There is no color at all here, the gray and dead void is full of countless gloomy and oppressive breaths, but there is still a vortex surging like the mouth of an evil beast.

This thing is a miniature version of the mouth of the outer world!

Glancing at this void, a trace of horror and unease filled Xiao Yan's heart.

Here, corpses are everywhere.

No, to be more precise, there are mountains of corpses all over the place, and countless cold and rotten corpses are placed here irregularly, randomly piled up into hundreds of corpse mountains over 100 meters long.

The number of corpses is probably in the millions, and blood is flowing everywhere, and it turns out to be slowly converging into a blood-colored river of hundreds of meters!

The blood river contains monstrous grievances, dead aura, and it seems to be directly connected to the evil giant mouth above this void, as if it supplies endless evil energy to it.

Broken bones, seeping human bones, can be seen everywhere.

The death energy soaring to the sky, the gloomy resentment was almost viscous to the point of substantiation, twisting the void, shaking Xiao Yan's mind, and he couldn't calm down for a long time.

This kind of scenery even surpasses all the events Xiao Yan has known before!

In order to arouse the mouth of Ming Yuan, the realm master Xuan Ming involved the ghost realm powerhouses into the underworld, thereby refining them into his loyal subordinates. No one knew how many souls he had killed.

This scene is like a real **** on earth.

"The evil giant mouth here... Could it be the manifestation of the mouth of the abyss outside?"

"Destroying it should be able to completely break the catastrophe."

Taking a heavy sigh of relief, Xiao Yan tried his best to calm down his mind, and then he could analyze the current situation and how to deal with it.

However, Xiao Yan still overestimated his character.

In such a doomsday hell, Xiao Yan's mind was always in some kind of tremor, his palms and limbs were shaking constantly, which seemed to be an instinctive fear.

The body does not obey, even the power of the Dragon God Transformation is very dim at this time by this monstrous death and resentment, and it will completely dissipate at any time.

"Help."

After resisting for ten minutes, his body and situation were still out of control, Xiao Yan could only close his eyes as best he could, and said in a solemn tone.

"Help!"

He snorted coldly, not knowing who Xiao Yan was talking to, and it kept echoing in this space.

In an instant, Xiao Yan's words were like a fuse that ignited a little bit, as if something that had slumbered him in the deepest part of his body slowly awakened.

"Boom!"

The power of the Dragon God Transformation was instantly pressed back into the body, and a monstrous force of incomparable madness slowly swept out.

With blood-colored blood-colored eyes and dark-green long hair, Xiao Yan has been completely filled with demonic power.

"I'm going to destroy it."

The cold voice of the demon Xiao Yan emerged, and the blood of the demon in his body immediately conveyed a touch of resistance.

The power of the devil seems to want Xiao Yan to obey the mouth of Ming Yuan and fall into the underworld through this passage.

"The underworld will go sooner or later, but not now."

"Although I don't know what a demon is, I think your demon factor and demon bloodline have already merged with me. You shouldn't want to die just like that."

Demon Xiao Yan muttered to himself, and there was no room for negotiation in his voice.

Once the mouth of the abyss spreads completely, not only the Li Soul Realm, but also countless ghost realms such as Soul Devouring Realm will all fall into it together.

To exchange the lives of nearly hundreds of millions of creatures for the entrance to the underworld is too heavy a price.

"Boom!"

In an instant, it seemed that Xiao Yan's words were provoked by Xiao Yan's words. Xiao Yan's skin and blood vessels suddenly became distorted, his face was hideous, and the blood of the devil in his body seemed to completely shatter the meridians at any time, and emerged, occupying Xiao Yan's blood. The body became another Xiao Yan.

"Help me... destroy it."

"In the future, I will let your devilish brilliance resound through every corner of the world."

"Otherwise... let's fall together."

No matter how heart-wrenching torture, Xiao Yan did not give in and roared with a hoarse expression. Such a determined expression actually slowly shook the greedy and evil demon power.

"Tear!"

A few minutes later, the power of resistance in the devil's power disappeared instantly. It also recognized Xiao Yan's words when it disappeared, and the sense of horror and fear in his body also disappeared instantly.

"Roar!"

The power of the devil eroded into the evil giant mouth above the void, but Xiao Yan's entire body shrank suddenly, as if he had touched some unstoppable evil power of heaven and earth.

"Shhhhhh!"

Under the obstruction of the demon Xiao Yan's almost self-mutilating method, the evil giant mouth above the sky seemed to be shrinking little by little, but Xiao Yan's body, bones, and even his soul disappeared little by little.

If this goes on and on, I am afraid that Xiao Yan will not be able to completely eradicate the evil giant, and he will completely fall here.

"Again... the last time."

"Ten Yan... I need you."

With a very miserable smile, the demon Xiao Yan said weakly.

The Seventh of the Ten Sacred Flames has been with Xiao Yan for a long time, but he has never seen Xiao Yan's expression like this, and immediately poured out of him little by little.

Ten holy flames, like the shadow of ten golden crows, quietly dormant beside Xiao Yan.

"Devil...Thank you."

Finally, the power of the demons was completely exhausted, and those demon bloodlines slowly poured into the depths of Xiao Yan's body as if they were withered, and lay dormant quietly.

"bring it on."

"Let me see, the true power of the mouth of the abyss!"

A crazy look appeared in his eyes, his palms slowly stretched out, and his body burned at a high speed.

"Boom!"

In an instant, the ten flames of the source of holy flame were tightly squeezed by Xiao Yan in the palm of his hand, and they began to gather little by little and get closer.

"Bang bang bang!"

The indescribable raging flames, like a fire **** descending, floated out of Xiao Yan's palm without reservation.

Blood and flesh were slowly scorched by this scorching flame that could kill everything, and became festering, and the vision became blurred together, as if it would be blown away by this unusually violent force at any time!

Xiao Yan's body cannot support the fusion of ten holy flames at all. If he rashly fuses , it may cause very serious consequences!

"Breakthrough!"

"Hmph... do you even want to laugh at me?"

Just under Xiao Yan's crazy actions, tens of thousands of noisy sounds erupted from the countless corpses all over his body.

Roaring, roaring, and wailing mixed together, directly shocked Xiao Yan's soul, as if he wanted to keep him here forever!

"Is it worth it?"

"You try so hard to help them get out of their misery, but they ruthlessly hate and laugh at you."

"Such creatures are really worth letting you sacrifice yourself to redeem them?"

"You... don't you think it's funny?"

Suddenly, a small figure appeared in the evil mouth, and then said with a touch of pity.

"As long as there is a little hope, it is not worth giving up."

Xiao Yan let out a low roar, like a wild beast.

"Xiao clan... what a bunch of weird guys."

The low sound reverberated through the entire mysterious space, and immediately, strands of demonic radiance rose up from Xiao Yan's body. Together with ten holy flames, he slowly gained the power to compete with the evil giant!
