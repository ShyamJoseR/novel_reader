It could also see that the main factor restricting its performance was this Golden Wheel, and every time it destroyed a profound level weapon, the power of the light wheel would weaken a bit.

“BOOM!”

Before the light wheel completely disappeared, the stone beast finally couldn’t withstand the bombardment of the crowd. Its entire chest exploded and turned into gravel. Then, starting from the head, it began to collapse from top to bottom, turning into small stones.
