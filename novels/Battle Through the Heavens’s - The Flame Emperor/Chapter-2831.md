This kind of terrifying existence is definitely not something that I can deal with now who is seriously injured. "A group of criminals... trespassing on the dragon tomb, this is a capital crime!"

"Tai Shang... It is these sinners who seriously injured Lord Long Feng."

The Dark Dragon King took a slight step, and the cold power of darkness permeated, and he was the first to speak.

That kind of dark power has a weak connection with the demon power in Xiao Yan's body, but at this time Xiao Yan obviously didn't have the heart to pay attention to this.

Even if he was controlled, deep in Zhu Kun's mind, he still clearly remembered this unforgettable name.

"Old opponent... I haven't had a chance to fight well, so I will be here today and fulfill my wish."

After Xiao Xuan finished speaking, the divine weapon Boundless Shield behind him slowly spread out, and the fiery fighting intent in his pupils could not be added.

At the beginning of the Dou Qi Continent, Xiao Xuan was the first person under Dou Di, and his achievements even surpassed those of Jinglian Yaosheng, Huangquan Yaosheng and others, but he was the only one who did not fight against this old dragon emperor.
