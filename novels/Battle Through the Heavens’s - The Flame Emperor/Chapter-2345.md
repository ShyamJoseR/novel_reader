# Chapter Vol 4 2296: The call of the spirit of the sycamore

After speaking, everyone started to move, walking towards the depths of this Phoenix Township.

"I don't know which holy flame this Phoenix family possesses?"

Xiao Yan was a little excited in his heart, but he didn't show it, because based on some obscure legends about the Phoenix family, Xiao Yan didn't think that the Phoenix family would give away the holy flame they guarded, and because the Phoenix family's secret methods were too meticulous , No one knows which holy flame is in their clan.

Even based on Xiao Yan's perception, these crystals and flames are not like vampires absorbing the ancient power in the plane trees... On the contrary, they are protecting these plane trees?

Bingxueyao knew that Bingfeng had the blood of the phoenix, so she could only instruct everyone.

After a while, everyone's vision suddenly became narrow, and a crystal forest almost surrounded by countless plane trees appeared in front of everyone without any sign.

The crystal forest is airtight and cannot use any force to penetrate the interior. A crystal passage is extremely conspicuous, and there are even some slight walking marks in this crystal passage, obviously someone has walked.

"help me."

When everyone hesitated, an extremely violent energy turned into fiery red energy ripples, spreading from the depths of the crystal forest in front of them, accompanied by an extremely weak sound.

"Did you hear that?"

Feng Qianjiao's body trembled abruptly, as if she could smell a real moving expression from this voice.

That is absolute suppression above the bloodline!

"This is……"

Obviously, the power to suppress the Seal of Samsara has reached its limit, and what Xun'er has to face next is the backlash of the Seal of Samsara, but there is still a tiny bit of time before the last step.

"help me."

The ancient low-pitched voice came out, and suddenly the ancient sycamore tree branches under the dust-covered crystal blocks in front of everyone flickered with fiery red light, and then suddenly exploded.

"This sycamore tree..."

"This is the guardian of Phoenix Township, the spirit of the phoenix tree!"

Although the Five-legged Golden Crow was suppressed by the Xuanniao Clan for many years, he was once the guy who followed the Nine Profound Fire Lord into the Phoenix Clan. His memory was floating and it was easy to recall.

"help me."

"The spirit of the plane tree, tell your request, we can help you!"

The void here is imprisoned and dusty, even with the strength of the five-legged Golden Crow's current triple reincarnation level, when it leaped into the air, a huge pressure caused its body to become twisted.

"Wong buzzing!"

Countless strong breaths of life rolled around in the lake, and the plane tree in front of him suddenly disappeared and turned into a little bit of fluorescent light scattered in this field, forming an illusory figure.
