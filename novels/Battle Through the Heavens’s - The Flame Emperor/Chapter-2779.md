This battle is not only to make them completely reincarnated, but also to rescue many strong men who have fallen into the sea of ​​reincarnation... Not only for others, but for themselves.

At this moment, many geniuses or some great powers in the Great Thousand World with the Reincarnation Seal are all swept towards the Reincarnation Sea area, and their purpose is self-evident.

At the same time, some unknown secrets are slowly unfolding in the reincarnation sea.

The location of the so-called Samsara Sea is mysterious. If there are no specific spatial coordinates, it is difficult for ordinary people to find it.

Reincarnation Sea, this is a gorgeous ocean standing in the vast sky. The whole body is long and winding, like a transparent Tianhe, in which the waves are sparkling, the water is colorful, the reincarnation breath is extremely thick, and the splendid energy and colors on it are fascinating. fan.

"Recently, the spatial energy fluctuations in the Sea of ​​Samsara are somewhat abnormal. What is the meaning of this?"

On the side of Samsara Sea, there are two halls facing each other out of thin air, and a faint and cold voice came from one of the halls.

Above this great hall, there are exquisite feathered silhouettes all over the body. Looking closely, it turns out that the so-called angels have the same effect!

This is a middle-aged woman. At this moment, she is sitting on the main hall with a calm face. Her posture is like catkins and jade, and the matching robes perfectly outline her slender and perfect jade body.

This person slowly swayed his wrist, gently opened the door of the main hall, and then stared at this reincarnation sea area in a daze, and there seemed to be some kind of fear and confusion in his deep eyes.

She guards countless reincarnations in the Sea of ​​Samsara, but she has never seen the tide of energy in the Sea of ​​Samsara so intense, so abnormal, that something big will happen.

"Did something big happen?"

The woman's eyes were slightly wrinkled, and she seemed to be puzzled.

"Liu Yu!"

And just when the latter was just about to make a move, there was a sudden rush of wind breaking from outside the gate of the palace.

"Hua Wei, what do you mean?"

Looking at the anxious middle-aged man who came quickly outside the temple gate, the woman looked at the Samsara Sea with beautiful eyes and asked softly.

"Liu Yu, you and I have each sent guards to the Sea of ​​Samsara for the Angel Kingdom and the central faction of the Human World. If anything goes wrong in the Sea of ​​Samsara, I'm afraid the two of us will not be able to escape the blame..."

Hua Wei said with a stern face, as if he had already sensed something.

"The affairs of the outside world have nothing to do with me. My duty is only to guard the sea of ​​reincarnation. If someone tries to harm the many powerhouses sleeping in the sea of ​​reincarnation, this seat will be the first to refuse!"

Liu Yu scolded coldly, and seemed to see through the latter's mind at a glance.

The first world war ended. The Sea of ​​Reincarnation was originally the last place of life before many strong men fell. It has been guarded by the kingdom of angels since ancient times, but the center of the human world has stepped in for this purpose. Their purpose is probably It was Xiao Yan, the great elder of the Xiao clan who was coveted to sleep in it!

However, Liu Yu is also a stubborn person, and naturally he will not let Hua Wei succeed so easily, so the two have guarded the reincarnation for several lifetimes here, and they have not given the latter any chance.

"This seat has received the latest information from the world master, and nearly half of the evil races from outside the territory have been sent to attack the Great Thousand World, and the Sea of ​​Samsara is the center of this catastrophe!"

"Quickly remove the reincarnation tombs of Xiao Yan, Xiao Lingshan and other adults, and find another place, otherwise a foreign army will surround the reincarnation sea, not only you and I will be buried here, these powerful people who have made great contributions to the world will also fall. Hands from outside the realm."

Hua Wei's face was bitter, as if she had good intentions.

"Don't worry about it, this seat has its own fixed number."

In the face of the latter's hard-hearted actions, Liu Yu slowly shook her head... In the whole world, it seems that she is the only one who knows the separation of the many reincarnations in the sea of ​​reincarnation. Ridiculous.

Just when the pressure on Liu Yu and Hua Wei increased greatly, a huge footprint suddenly stepped out in the abyss, and an indifferent voice without any emotion, instantly enveloped the entire Samsara region!

Even those powerhouses who were secretly sleeping in the sea of ​​reincarnation seemed to have suddenly released a wave of frightening fluctuations because of the power of their instincts!
