# Chapter Vol 4 2361: Void mansion, farewell!

For a month, Xiao Yan had been living in the stone room to calmly regulate the many powers in his body, and even missed the mighty Poison God festival in the Poison God Palace.

However, I gained a lot of insights because of this, and I became more and more proficient in the strength of my double reincarnation rank.

"The double reincarnation level is really not comparable to the first level."

Xiao Yan stretched out his hand and clenched his fist, feeling the extreme surging power of destruction permeating, and couldn't help but laugh.
