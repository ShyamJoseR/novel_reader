Get a keel that suits you and use it to increase your strength.

The Orangutan Fist Bone, which was transformed after the fall of such a strong man, has always had a very high value among the Dragon Clan, and it was unexpected that it would be obtained by Longyuan now.

"Your opponent is me."

When Long Yi was distracted, Zhu Kun burst out with a loud shout, and the sharp reincarnation breath permeated out, directly fighting the latter.

However, Zhu Kun at this time is completely different from the old Dragon Emperor period when he was on Dou Qi Continent. During the Dou Qi Continent period, he was undoubtedly stronger countless times.

"Dragon Palm!"

In the face of Zhu Kun's ruthless attack, Long Yi's pressure was greatly increased for a while, and even those who were attacked by the latter had many large and small scars on his body.
