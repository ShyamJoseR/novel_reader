By the time the planet completed its rotation and the travel logistics were finalized, the countdown would organically drop back to 4 days, yet Ren would have already arrived at the battlefield operating at absolute peak capacity.

It was a remarkably tight, highly demanding schedule, yet it flawlessly accounted for every single moving piece on the board.
