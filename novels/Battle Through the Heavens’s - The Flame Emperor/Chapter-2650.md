# Chapter Vol 4 2598: Fantasy or test?

"The space here is... so weird."

The void in front of him was changing, sometimes illusory, and sometimes weird, but it made Xiao Yan feel uneasy... as if he was in the belly of some giant creature!

In the void in front of him, scenes of mountain peaks, bamboo forests, islands and other scenery are constantly changing and mysterious in front of him, and they also contain endless mystery.

In this regard, Xiao Yan and the three of them have been quietly standing on the spot, squinting their eyes and watching everything around them. They will not act rashly until they have figured out the specific situation.

"This is indeed the center of the demon world... but I don't know much about it."

There was a seriousness on Tian Xie Xue Hu's face, obviously from the changing and passing pictures in front of him, there were many terrifying existences that could threaten his life.

"Although this void does not have any energy from heaven and earth, there is a very incomprehensible force that is interfering with my perception, and it seems to be deliberately blocking our progress."

Xiao Yan shook his head, not rushing forward.

This world is too weird, you have to be careful!

And judging from the news left by the demon emperor, the position of this guy in the center of the demon world is unclear, and he must take every step of the way!

"go."

Cai Lin's expression became calm, and under the leadership of the snake spirit above her shoulders, she stepped directly into the void in front of her.

Obviously, the closer it is to the hometown of the Ten-Colored Sky Swallowing Python, the memory and power of the snake spirit are also getting stronger.

"Bang!"

Cailin's body had just stepped into the void, and the ever-changing void in front of her was easily shocked by the snake spirit, but it became calm, and disappeared after just a moment.

What caught my eye was an endless calm lake.

The lake was shimmering with sparkling waves, but upon scanning it carefully, it was not difficult to find that around the lake were lined with magnificent palaces that were somewhat illusory.

Among those palaces, it seems that each of them has some kind of ancient, powerful aura, which is daunting!

"Where's the temple of the master of the center of the demon world..."

Looking at the nearly ten mysterious temples in front of him, Xiao Yan only felt a shock in his heart.

No matter how he motivated his soul power, he could not detect any of the temples at this time.

It seems that these temples in front of them seem to be illusory and no longer exist, but they are real after watching.

The truth and the fake made Xiao Yan's perception confused.

"The core of the lake?"

At this time, Cailin Meimei stared at the gray palace in the depths of the lake that was haunted by demonic energy, and seemed to smell a subtle fluctuation from it.

"go!"

Just after finishing speaking, the three of them moved on the lake and swept towards the temple deep in the lake. While moving, they actually saw a strange picture from above the clear and transparent lake surface.

In the picture, there is a strange mountain peak, one of which is a large round platform of dozens of feet, and a huge rock stands in it.

On the top of the round platform, there is a hole about the size of a fist. The hole vaguely forms a mysterious hexagon. The dark aura is constantly pouring out of it, but there is no trace of overflowing, and it constantly surrounds the boulder.

His eyes narrowed slightly, but this boulder began to emit a strange light, like an ancient prohibition!

On top of the boulder, in addition to the black energy that doesn't know what to do with it, you can vaguely see the patterns of nine monsters flickering in it...

There is no shortage of dragons, tigers... wolves and other three recognized monsters in the monster world. They have contemptuous expressions and extremely strange expressions, and they vaguely form some kind of connection.

It seems to be guarding something, and at the same time being alert to some hostile enemies.

"This is... the entrance to the hometown of the Ten-Colored Sky Swallowing Python?"

I don't know why this picture appeared on the lake, but after a while, the snake spirit on Cailin's shoulder suddenly vibrated, and a vigilant and fiery aura filled out at the same time.

However, the snake eyes of the snake spirit were not looking at the lake, but raised their head and looked at the sky.

"Boom!"

With a sudden sound, the sound of fragments appeared, the lake returned to calm again, and Xiao Yan and the others also came to this temple in an instant.

In front of the temple, the silver brilliance adorned it little by little, setting it off beautifully.

"go!"

At this time, Xiao Yan did not hesitate, his speed skyrocketed, and he went directly to the main entrance of the temple.

No matter what the attitude of the world master in the center of the demon world is towards them, the hometown of the Ten-Colored Sky Swallowing Python must go!

And the homeland of the Ten-Colored Sky Swallowing Python also exists in the center of the demon world. No matter what, this trip can't get around the world master of the demon world, so it falls into an infinite loop.

"Pfft."

The ancient portal was quietly pushed open, and a heavy, dusty aura emerged.

Just this faint ray of energy, that kind of fluctuation is on a par with the evil blood tiger!

"This is not where you should come... Exit."

Immediately, in the main entrance of the temple, a strange white halo emerged, accompanied by a magnificent sky-dang sound in the white world.

"The real main hall is under your feet and in your heart."

With a little bit of it, a strange demon light instantly cut through the void and led Xiao Yan and the three away from the place.

"Nine-tailed demon fox..."

Before leaving, Tian Xie Xue Hu also suddenly realized the powerhouse inhabiting this temple!

Among the 100,000 territories of the demon world, the ancestor of the nine-tailed demon fox clan!

Legend has it that this guy's strength has already stood at the fifth-layer reincarnation level in the first World War. After countless years, he did not fall, but stepped into the center of the demon world.

Now her strength, I am afraid, is so powerful that it is astonishing.

"Under our feet... in our hearts."

Combining with the ten-colored snake spirit and the voice in the temple just now, Xiao Yan seemed to be touched and kept muttering.

"The Lord of the Endless Fire Realm, Xiao Yan...comes to visit the Demon Realm Realm Lord."

"..."

"Haha... Was the test of illusions resolved so easily?"

It seems that there is no power that can resist the extremely domineering power, so he can only hibernate quietly and let it be slaughtered!

"He's going to kill us!"

At this time, Tian Xie Xuehu was shocked, because he felt an unavoidable chilling aura from the substantive demonic energy around him!

Even if Xiao Yan possessed holy flames and tyrannical weapons, he would never be able to make up for this huge gap like a gulf!

For a time, the whole strange void was filled with a dead silence. It seemed that under such an attack, the bodies and even the souls of Xiao Yan and the three would easily fall to the ground.

All forms and spirits are destroyed... forever!
