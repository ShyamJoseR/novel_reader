In the current situation, Yunxiao could either be killed or admit defeat. Either way was extremely beneficial to him, so he could not let anyone ruin the battle.

After Yi Hongming had burned his blood, his aura grew stronger and stronger. With just a raise of his hand, he had the power to destroy the world, forcing Yunxiao to flee in all directions.
