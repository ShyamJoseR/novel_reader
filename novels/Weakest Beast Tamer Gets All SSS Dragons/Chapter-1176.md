# Chapter 1176 - Taming the Old World - Eternal Hope - 4

There was a second, far more subtle reason this redeployment was even possible.

The vast majority of the outer perimeter was not facing the suffocating volume of Platinum mutants that Taro's sector was currently enduring. The flanks were only dealing with sporadic, isolated Platinums mixed into seas of some Golds with mostly silver fodder.

They were nuisance attacks, scattered pressure points designed solely to break Ren's towers and paralyze the Vanguard and prevent the commanders from grouping up.

The defensive grid was holding entirely because the sister cities now boasted a massive, highly competent middle class of high-tier Gold Tamers. These young soldiers were holding the line using the exact same optimized cultivation methods Ren had freely donated to the public and all other academies years ago. The seeds of his generosity had finally blossomed into an army capable of surviving this level of enemy.

The true, overwhelming push, the bigger concentration of Platinum horrors, was focused entirely on this exact coordinate, aiming straight for Yino Castle.

Now that High Command fully understood Selthia's true objective, they could afford to stop fighting blind. They no longer needed to spread all their strongest pieces dangerously thin across the entire continent. They could consolidate some of their remaining firepower exactly where it was needed most.

And so, the heavy cavalry arrived.

Even Yang managed to break away from the subterranean trenches.

His original assignment had him anchoring the underground grid near Victor's old sector. With the line consolidated, he found the margin to surface. And Yang's arrival was not merely a minor boost.

When his boots hit the stone, the earth groaned. His signature fusion, a breathtaking amalgamation of a heavy-plated Stone Behemoth and his towering Royal Gold Treant, was an absolute spectacle of destruction.

In practical combat terms, Yang 2 cloned beasts had mutated into the mana-biological equivalent of one of Ren's autonomous wooden towers, only this ones operated at Platinum rank power level.

He was a living, breathing mountain of impenetrable iron-stone, interwoven with indestructible golden muscle and thick, golden metallic roots. He stepped into the breach and simply refused to yield, catching the charge of three Platinum mutants simultaneously. He possessed the sheer, immovable density of a fortress, completely equipped with its own crushing will.

Yang's fusion wasn't forged from the impossible adamantium Ren was about to build. It lacked that logic-defying, elemental resistance.

But it was more than enough to act as a physical dam. He was exactly what the bleeding frontline desperately required: a towering, unyielding mass capable of plugging the gaping hole Ren was about to leave behind.

And flanking him was Lin. She needed absolutely no introduction.

Lin had already proven her lethality long ago, casually dissecting low-tier Platinum beasts entirely on her own during the nightmare descent into Sirius's chasm.

She was the Vanguard's ultimate wild card.

She operated as a flawless support executioner, capable of seamlessly plugging into whatever gap the frontline exposed, reinforcing failing walls, and holding her ground against almost any horror the corruption threw her way.

With her powerful presence entering the fray, the containment line suddenly possessed a margin of error that simply hadn't existed some minutes ago.

Ren ran the problems in his head one final time, processing the shifting variables with great speed.

Zhao locking down the airspace. Victor freezing the flanks. Lin executing the stragglers. Yang physically anchoring the center like a living monolith. And a small army of highly trained Gold Tamers holding the distant perimeters.

They finally knew exactly where the hammer was falling.

It was still an incredibly precarious position. The commanders were fighting through heavy exhaustion. They were still severely outnumbered, and Selthia's terrifying second supply line was currently marching toward them through the dark.

But they could hold. They had the output to weather the storm and wait for the dawn.

For the very first time since he had dropped from the sky, Ren looked at his friends and felt the answer to the suffocating question, 'can they survive without me for ten minutes?' was a definitive yes.

"Hold the line, then," Ren ordered.

He instantly severed his combat fusion, aggressively pulling his sprawling aura back into his core to preserve every single remaining drop of ether. His human features returned, stark against the soot and flashing elemental light.

"Buy me the time... I will permanently seal the entrance beneath the castle."

Placing his absolute trust in the bruised, bleeding soldiers standing beside him, the Glorious General turned his back to the rotting tide and sprinted toward the towering gates of Yino.

He moved.

Building the defensive tower was not going to be a simple, rapid architectural project.

Ren was attempting to mold a metal that, according to every known law of elemental physics, should have been completely outside his capacity to manipulate.

The colossal fragment of pseudo-adamantium currently resting in his shadow space was fundamentally denser than diamond rank. Trying to bend, shape, and hollow out a block of that caliber wasn't a matter of simple willpower. It was the magical equivalent of asking a sculptor to bend a thick steel girder using nothing but hands made of water.

Furthermore, he wasn't just building a hollow shell. He intended to weave his absolute highest-quality materials, premium Platinum-rank ironwood, hyper-dense roots, and mana-rich vines, into the inner core of the metal chassis.

To ensure the organic heartbeat of the tower didn't instantly vaporize under the crushing pressure of the adamantium shell, he had to forcefully upgrade the plants. He needed to push the plant matter beyond its natural ceiling, elevating it to Diamond-tier potency so it could decently interface with the metal.

Under normal circumstances, achieving any of this in the middle of a chaotic battlefield would have been a laughable impossibility.

But Ren knew he could pull it off. He had spent years meticulously stacking an absurd accumulation of hidden advantages, and right now, he was calling every single one of those markers in.

He stopped directly in front of the massive castle gates. He raised his hands and unleashed everything.
