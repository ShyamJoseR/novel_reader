# Chapter Vol 4 2752: decision making

"I don't know much about the underworld. After all, before the end of the first world war, I fell into a deep sleep in reincarnation. In the end, the Xiao family declined or even disappeared. I don't know the specifics."

Seeing everyone's impatient gaze, Elder Xiao Yan also spoke lightly.

Regarding this matter, the powerhouses such as Yama of the Tenth Palace, Feng Wan'er, and King Yaoji of Swallowing Spirit also looked at each other, which was obviously true.

"But... I don't recommend going to the underworld for rescue at this time."

"The two superpowers in the center of the human world, the kingdom of angels, both control the power to destroy the sky and destroy the earth. Now their situation is unclear. If we make a rash move, once we are besieged by them and the evil tribes outside the territory, I am afraid it will not be worth the loss."

"The most important thing... my strength is different from ordinary reincarnation, and it will take some time to fully recover."

"When I recover completely, cooperate with Lei Master's awakening, and Yao Ji, nearly three ninth-level reincarnation-level powerhouses lead the team, is the best time to enter the underworld."

Elder Xiao Yan pondered for a while, and then expressed his concerns.

This kind of approach is undoubtedly the safest... After all, the underworld has too many things and forces involved. If you go there rashly, it is very easy to trap the strong Xiao clan in a dangerous place if you don't handle it properly.

"What the first elder said is very true, the recovery of power is not easy in the nine-fold reincarnation hierarchy."

"And according to the Lord of Soul Eater, the reincarnation of Master Lei's master and apprentice has been loosened, and I am afraid that the reincarnation will be successful soon."

Yao Ji also agrees with this.

"There seems to be something strange in the underworld. The existence of that kind of thing hinders the attack and exploration of the angel kingdom and the center of the human world. If we can't solve this obstacle, I'm afraid..."

After a while, Yama of the Tenth Hall and King Swallowing Spirit spoke at the same time. Obviously, they still have a lot of knowledge about the underworld.

"Dark matter..."

"With me, there's nothing to worry about."

After hesitating for a while, Xiao Yan decided to give everyone a reassurance.

The existence of the power of the devil makes it impossible for the dark matter to cause any hindrance to it. After all, the power of the devil seems to originate from the underworld.

"That's good."

"Everyone, go back to rectify the forces first... The day of the real decisive battle is not far away."

During the conversation, he also made a decision to keep the same, Xiao Yan just said.

For a while, the meeting that gathered the top executives of the Endless Flame Alliance quietly came to an end.

That night.

The night is already deep, and the breeze blows the Buddha, making the moonlight soft and beautiful.

"Elder, are you looking for me?"

At this time, in a quiet courtyard in the Endless Fire Territory, Xiao Yan quietly pushed open the door and looked at the elder Xiao Yan who was sitting on it, now drinking fine wine alone.

"sit down."

"I came to you, of course I have something important to do."

Xiao Yan raised the wine glass like raw jade and drank it. Immediately, a fiery red energy barrier spread out from his body, and he easily sheltered the entire courtyard tightly. The terrifying fluctuations contained in the barrier, It is not something that ordinary strong people can crack.

"What advice does the senior have?"

Seeing this, Xiao Yan also raised his glass and drank it together, thinking quickly in his heart.

"The weapon of the Holy King, the ruler of the gods, should be in your hands, right?"

After the enchantment completely enveloped this place, Xiao Yan's facial expression suddenly condensed slightly, and he said in a low voice.

"..."

Xiao Yan did not respond, but his eyes suddenly filled with doubts.

The matter of Xiao Tianyuan receiving the Yanshen Cauldron at the beginning made Xiao Yan extremely vigilant about foreign objects in his heart, and the execution of the Godly Ruler was a very important matter, and he could not tolerate the slightest sloppiness.

"This is the birthmark of the Xiao family, which cannot be forged. With its proof, you should know that I am the real Xiao Yan."

"If you don't believe my identity, just check."

Seeing Xiao Yan who was unmoved, the first elder was also surging, and the Xiao clan pattern on the eyebrows fell off instantly and flew towards Xiao Yan.

Such important clan lines fall off at will, I am afraid that such a big man can be so casual.

"It is indeed my Xiao clan pattern... This thing cannot be faked."

After three confirmations, Xiao Yan slowly took out the God-Execution Ruler.

"The Execution Ruler is indeed in my hands, but now it is only a semi-finished product, and its power is not as strong as before. With my strength, I have only completed the first stage of tempering... According to the information I have obtained, I want to To complete the second stage of tempering, there must be a strong person with strength above the Nine Reincarnation Rank."

"Please forgive the vigilance of the younger generation, the elders, after all, this matter is of great importance."

After some remarks, Xiao Yan also smiled bitterly.

Xiao Tianyuan's affairs made Xiao Yan vigilant about many things around him. After all, judging from some obscure traces at that time, Xiao Tianyuan's fall seems to be the poison of someone close to him... Of course, there is no evidence. These are all speculations by Xiao Yan alone, but caution is essential.

"It's okay... Calling you at this time, I really have something to say."

"After tonight, I need to go out to restore my peak strength. I will take away the ruler of the gods and successfully repair it... This will be an indispensable strength in our battle in the underworld."

"And you... need to go to the Dragon Clan to help the Golden Dragon King resolve the Dragon Clan's civil strife."

"At that time, with the help of our three nine-level reincarnation-level powerhouses and the dragon clan, we will only be able to increase our calculations for a victory in the underworld."

Elder Xiao Yan spoke lightly, as if he had already made up his mind.

He knows that once the battle of the underworld begins, I am afraid that the powerhouses that the Endless Flame Alliance will face will be the three major forces outside the realm, the center of the human world, and even the kingdom of angels!

The three major forces alone will definitely not be a fuel-efficient lamp. If they are united, they will easily put the Endless Flame Alliance to death.

"..."

Xiao Yan did not answer, the power of the God-Execution Ruler and the Devil are now his two major killers. If the God-Execution Ruler is taken away by the elder Xiao Yan and goes to the Dragon Clan by himself, it may be very difficult.

But if this is not the case, the future battle of the underworld will become much more difficult.

On the one hand, there is the Golden Dragon King who has the grace to know him, on the other hand, there is the mystery of the family, which makes Xiao Yan's mind slightly heavy for a while.

"Now that you are at the peak of the eighth-level reincarnation, entering the ninth-level reincarnation is just around the corner, but you are missing a chance to touch the threshold of the ninth-level reincarnation. The trip to the dragon family is a must."

"This thing, given to you, can be regarded as compensation for you. With it, the trip to the dragon family will be much more convenient."

While speaking, a fiery red flame suddenly appeared in front of Xiao Yan.

He stretched out his hand slightly, and a fierce burning sensation was transmitted from the palm of his hand, as if there was some kind of terrifying burning aura that was not inferior to the holy flame.

"what is this?"

Based on Xiao Yan's current perception, he could not find the mystery of this thing after some investigation, so he carefully dragged the flame with his palm to change groups and asked.
