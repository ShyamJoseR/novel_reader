# Chapter Vol 4 2761: The two sides are fighting!

At night, when the world around the dragon tomb fell into darkness, a mighty dragon coalition, led by the Dragon King Baxia and others, quietly burst towards the dragon tomb.

The purpose of this trip is very clear, to provoke the defenders left by the Silver Dragon King, secretly **** Xiao Yan into it, and rescue the Golden Dragon King!

"Boom!"

In the dim world, outside the originally peaceful dragon tomb, countless gunpowder smoke suddenly gushed out of the deadly air, causing the many powerhouses guarding the dragon tomb to look at each other, and the pressure increased for a while.

"My lord, I found that a thousand-person team is rapidly approaching the Dragon Tomb, and it is aggressive."

"The leaders... are the Baxia Dragon King and the Fire Dragon King."

Suddenly, on a small hill outside the dragon tomb, the dragon warriors who were responsible for investigating the information kept talking.

"Sir, do you see?"

As the voice of the dragon warrior fell, the other people who had been quietly guarding here couldn't help but turn their attention to the leader.

The Supreme Elder of the Dragon Clan was ordered by the Silver Dragon King and was the supreme commander of the coalition forces here.

As for the next ones, they are followed by the four dragon kings who submit to the silver dragon king, the dark dragon king, the earth dragon king, the storm dragon king and the star dragon king.

"Dark Dragon King, Earth Dragon King, Storm Dragon King, Star Dragon King, now our lives are all tied to a boat. If this matter is completely exposed, with the powerful skills of the Golden Dragon King, then you should know the end better than me."

As he spoke, the Supreme Elder's silent eyes glanced over the bodies of several people, sweeping away the last concerns in his heart.

"we know."

"I swear to guard the dragon's tomb to the death on this trip. Anyone who tries to intrude will be killed without mercy...even the two dragon kings."

When the four Dragon Kings heard the words, they also nodded solemnly. Ever since they joined the Silver Dragon King faction, they knew that such a day would come sooner or later, but they did not expect it to be so fast.

Once the Silver Dragon King succeeds, the situation of the Dragon Clan will drastically change. At that time, the opposition within the Dragon Clan will also be suppressed by the Silver Dragon King.

"Go ahead and meet them."

"Yes."

As soon as the words fell, the atmosphere here became extremely tense, and an extremely hot force swept across the void of the Dragon Tomb.

"Boom!"

When the elder Taishang and the four dragon kings appeared in front of the Dragon Tomb Alliance together, the Baxia Dragon King and others were already ready to fight.

"Baxia Dragon King, Fire Dragon King, you wait for such a move, but you want to disobey the order of the Silver Dragon King?"

The elder Taishang stood among the defenders of Tailong Tomb, and looked at the other's thousand troops with a calm expression, and his tone was full of contempt.

The thousands of troops of the two dragon kings and the defenders behind him are not at the same level of combat power, let alone the existence of the four great dragon kings and his supreme elder on his side. The combat power of both sides is too high. disparity.

With the intelligence of the Baxia Dragon King, naturally he would not do such a thing that burns all the jade and stone. What is the reason for these guys to have such courage to attack the dragon tomb?

"It's all nonsense. The land of the dragon's tomb is the most forbidden place for the dragon clan. Only our nine dragon kings are qualified to enter it. Isn't the sacredness here that you blood-mottled dragons can touch?"

"If you know each other, get out of here quickly."

The Dragon King of Baxia came forward directly and stood at the front of the team. The provocative fluctuations between the words became more and more intense.

"You are the Dragon King, which is the foundation of my dragon clan. With time, you will be able to cultivate to the peak of this world. Why should you die in vain?"

"The current situation of the Dragon Clan is already beyond your control... This old man is unwilling to kill each other, so you should retreat quickly."

In the face of the provocation of the two dragon kings, the Supreme Elder shook his head slightly, obviously also a general knowledgeable person.

Now coincides with the catastrophe of the Great Thousand Worlds, if the dragon clan's civil strife loses too much combat power, even if the dragon clan completes the reunification, it will not regain its former glory.

"Hmph, old miscellaneous hair, as Baxia said, all of this is your conspiracy."

In an instant, the Fire Dragon King on the side looked at Elder Taishang and the others with such a pointed tone, and he was also angry and shouted directly.

"Where is there any conspiracy, what we do is also for the dragon family."

"After this is done, you will naturally understand the good intentions of my Lord."

The elder Taishang continued.

"For the Dragon Clan?"

"Just relying on such insidious means like you, you have the words to mention the dragon family?"

"The status of the overlord of the dragon race will be destroyed in your hands sooner or later."

For a time, Cailin and Yunyun quietly walked out of the crowd and stood beside the two dragon kings together.

"Where is the little girl from, with sharp teeth and sharp mouth."

Seeing the appearance of the two mistresses, there was a hint of surprise in the eyes of the Taishang elders. Obviously, there was a slight difference in the identities and strengths of the two.

"Stop talking nonsense, let's do it!"

"Today we must enter the dragon tomb!"

The four looked at each other, and immediately spoke with the Dragon King and others.

"kill!"

In the face of such a menacing attack from the other party, the elder Taishang also gave an order, and the war is about to break out!

"Boom!"

When the two powerhouses were fighting together, a splendid streamer instantly rose from the army behind Baxia Dragon King and others, and immediately rushed into the melee like lightning, swept towards the entrance of the Dragon Tomb. go.

"Are you smart..."

In this regard, the elder Taishang seemed to have been prepared for a long time, and when his body moved, he chased away in an instant.

"Yunyun, you stay here to help the army deal with the four dragon kings, I'll stop him!"

Just finished speaking, the colorful halo around Cailin's body is in full bloom, and the whole body is surging, and there is even a looming nine-colored light on it, which has the demeanor of a ruthless queen.

The Supreme Elder must not be allowed to block Xiao Yan's path, let alone consume Xiao Yan's strength, after all, Xiao Yan now has a heavy responsibility.

"Saint Dragon Finger!"

A gloomy voice resounded through the sky, and immediately a yellow finger of light with a size of several tens of meters pierced through the void and blasted away at Xiao Yan.

"Crack."

Even with Xiao Yan's excellent soul power and agility in his movements, it was a little difficult to flash such an attack.

That light refers to the energy around him, still making the tyrant dragon scales on Xiao Yan's body emit a burnt smell.

Although this guy's strength is also in the half-step nine-fold reincarnation rank, his combat power is comparable to that of the hundred-legged evil emperor in the reincarnation sea.

"Where's the younger generation?"

As soon as the hit was in the air, there was a flash of surprise in the eyes of the elder Taishang. With his full strength, even if the opponent was a real half-step ninth-level reincarnation-level powerhouse, he could not easily deal with his blow. Why is this roaring light in front of him? Can the breath in the middle be dealt with so easily?

Where is the other party sacred?

"Your opponent, is me."

When the Supreme Elder was chasing Xiao Yan away, Cailin suddenly appeared beside the latter, and the ten-colored snake spirit continued to swallow the snake letter. Obviously, this person in front of him was a really troublesome being.

"Ten-colored swallowing python?"

"It's somewhat qualified to be my opponent."

The elder Taishang sneered, but his eyes were still locked on the fast-moving light group.

If at this critical juncture, the other party steps into the dragon tomb, once the Silver Dragon King fails, their ending will naturally fall into a state of doom.

Today, they have no place to turn around, and they can only fight against the water, even if they are burdened with heavy infamy.
