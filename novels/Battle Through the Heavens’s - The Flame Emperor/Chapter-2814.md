# Chapter Vol 4 2758: dragon tomb obscure

"The dragon clan has always been headed by the golden dragon king, but after the first world war, the golden dragon king disappeared for several years, so the dragon clan fell into the hands of the silver dragon king."

"The Silver Dragon King has always been dissatisfied with the Golden Dragon King. In the control of the Dragon Clan, he secretly eradicated heretics and gradually controlled the entire Dragon Clan in his own hands. Unfortunately, the Golden Dragon King came back one day, and he wanted to get everything back."

"In an instant, those staunch factions hidden in the dragon clan defied all opinions and supported the golden dragon king, but the silver dragon king refused to accept it... So far, the dragon clan civil strife has begun."

"The Golden Dragon King faction and the Silver Dragon King faction broke out countless fights during the civil strife, and the strong on both sides were injured, but no matter how fierce the battle was, or even the crisis, in the end, the Golden Dragon King was forced to rely on one's strength. Mix the battle to a tie."

"Although the Silver Dragon King faction has the support of the five dragon kings, the Fire Dragon King and the Baxia Dragon King under the Golden Dragon King are the best among the seven dragon kings, so the battle situation has been anxious."

The wooden dragon king's expression was full of resentment, and he said word by word.

"Silver Dragon King... have foreign aid been invited?"

At this moment, a bad premonition suddenly appeared in Xiao Yan's heart. Could it be that the dragon clan of the Great Thousand World is like the Taixu Ancient Dragon on the Dou Qi Continent?

"not at all."

"The rest, it's up to me."

Xiao Xuan patted Xiao Yan's shoulder, then turned around and said.

"We have fought many times, and the Silver Dragon King watched the Golden Dragon King's terrifying power gradually recover, and slowly became unable to sit still, because she knew that if time went on like this, she would lose power sooner or later, so she would sleep in the Dragon Clan. The elder Taishang woke up and persuaded the latter at a great price to help him unify the dragon clan."

"With the support and help of the Supreme Elder and the lineage of the five dragon kings, the power of the lineage of the golden dragon king gradually declined."

"In addition, in several battles, although the Golden Dragon King has experienced hundreds of battles, he has suffered a lot of hidden illnesses because of this, so he is ready to step into the dragon tomb, find the law of the dragon god, break through the current shackles, and use the extremely strong Power out, trying to end the dragon civil unrest."

"As for me, I originally wanted to go out for help, but I didn't expect..."

Xiao Xuan opened his mouth and explained what he knew.

"What kind of rank is that Supreme Elder...?"

Hearing this, Xiao Yan also had a repressed emotion in his heart. The elders of the Dragon Clan, such power, may not be a simple thing.

"Eight... The eighth level of reincarnation is the pinnacle!"

The wooden dragon king's eyes turned slightly, as if he was a thief, trembling with fear. As for Xiao Xuan and the others, they didn't know much about it.

"The peak of the eighth-level reincarnation?"

When Cai Lin heard the words, although she didn't open her mouth, a suspicion flashed in her cold beautiful eyes.

Judging from some memories of the Ten-Colored Sky Swallowing Python, there are more than one elders of the dignified dragon clan, and their strength may not be a simple peak of the eight-fold reincarnation.

"I've said everything I know, can you let me go?"

Looking at the contemplative people in front of him, the Wood Dragon King also hurriedly spoke.

"No."

Just when Xiao Yan was about to get rid of him, Xiao Xuan waved his hand slowly.

"The nine dragon kings are said to be transformed by the power of the dragon gods. If any of them are lost, it is not a good thing for the dragon clan... Moreover, with your current strength, it is absolutely impossible to completely obliterate a dragon king. Easy thing."

Xiao Xuan softly echoed in Xiao Yan's ear and said that killing a Dragon King is truly terrifying.

"The death penalty can be avoided, but the living crime can't be escaped!"

"Forbidden Temple, seal it for me!"

After hesitating for a while, Xiao Yan used the power of reincarnation domain and holy flame in his body to form a gray palace with a size of dozens of meters, and instantly suppressed the wooden dragon king.

"This suppression, the strength is lower than the peak of the eighth-layer reincarnation, it cannot be opened, and it will disintegrate on its own within a month."

"Elder Xiao Xuan, take me to the Dragon Tomb as soon as possible."

The next moment, Xiao Yan couldn't help clenching his fists, and there was an undisguised killing intent in his eyes.

"Sir Xiao Yan, go this way, this is the closest place to the Dragon Tomb... But along the way, you may encounter many enemies of the Silver Dragon King faction."

"The land of the Dragon Tomb is now heavily guarded, and we need to go to another place."

"go."

As soon as the words fell, the group leaped across the sky in a mighty manner and flew towards the land of the dragon tomb.

On the other side, at the place of the Dragon Tomb.

At this time, the huge dragon tomb was surrounded by countless dragon warriors and seemed to be guarding something abnormal.

"Excuse me."

"How's the situation with the Wood Dragon King?"

"Lord Wood Dragon King has already locked the trail of Xiao Xuan and others, and I'm afraid they will be arrested soon."

"That's good."

"We just need to guard here, everything is fixed."

As soon as the voice fell, the Supreme Elder also slowly closed his eyes and turned into a very sparse and ordinary figure, hiding among the dragon warriors.

"Shhhhh!"

Suddenly, a piercing sound of breaking wind resounded through the heavens and the earth, and two figures with gloomy faces arrived here one after the other.

"What are you doing?"

As soon as the two of them arrived here, they looked at the heavily guarded soldiers around the Dragon Tomb, and their expressions immediately froze.

"I'm waiting for something important, and I need to enter the ground of the dragon tomb to meet the Golden Dragon King, so why don't you retreat quickly?"

Looking at the group of dragon warriors surrounding the dragon tomb, the Dragon King Baxia condensed and scolded directly.

"Dragon tomb is a forbidden place for the dragon family, so I can't let it go."

These dragon warriors were obviously well-trained, and they refused directly.

"After delaying the important event of the dragon race, can you afford such a risk?"

In an instant, beside the Dragon King Baxia, a man with long red hair cursed in a fiery temper.

"Hehe, Fire Dragon King, Baxia Dragon King, why are you so resolute?"

"We are under the order of Lord Silver Dragon King to guard the dragon tomb day and night. Without the order of the Silver Dragon King, even the orders of the two Dragon Kings may not be able to pass."

Suddenly, a white-haired old man slowly walked out of the crowd, looking at the two with a smile on his face.

The moment it appeared, the energy of heaven and earth around the entire dragon tomb solidified invariably, and a huge uneasiness permeated the hearts of the two dragon kings.

"you……"

The flame above the red hair of the Fire Dragon King has a kind of irritable fluctuation, but just after he made a move, he was stopped by the Dragon King next to him.

"Let's go, go back first."

In an instant, the Baxia Dragon King and the Fire Dragon King disappeared together, but their eyes changed, obviously worrying about something.

"Elder Taishang, why don't you take them down?"

Several surrounding dragon warrior leaders leaned over and asked.

"In the civil strife of the dragon clan, many strong people have been lost. Now the Lord Silver Dragon King has gone deep into the dragon tomb. As long as the latter succeeds, they will naturally understand who is the real king among the dragon clan."

When the voice fell, calm was restored here, but under the dead silence, there were many dangers hidden.
