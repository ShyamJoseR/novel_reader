# Chapter Vol 4 2291: mourners in snow

"In front is the Storm Mountains... This is the place where the Phoenix Stone is most likely to exist."

"Let's go here and have a look first."

The three of them gritted their teeth and marched to the end of the plain in the strong wind. Seeing the towering peaks in front of them, a dazed breath emerged in their hearts.

"Most likely?"

Xiao Yan took Xun'er's palm and looked up. The mountains in front of him were filled with an aura of awe. It seemed that there were many tyrannical creatures inhabiting them, and it was daunting from a distance.

"The wasteland... the ravines are rugged... the icebergs and snow."

"Is this ancient tree in the sky a treasure, or a place guarding the entrance of the Phoenix family, why does it have such a strange climate area..."

"Feng Qian, where is the least likely place for the Phoenix Stone to exist?"

After a while, Xun'er's Reincarnation Seal exuded a gentle touch, so she came up with a whim.

"It's the snow-capped peak behind us."

"But this is the most dangerous place."

Feng Qian was a little puzzled. She didn't know why Xun'er asked, but she didn't say much because of the existence of divine weapons and holy flames on her body.

"Xun'er, have you found anything?"

Xiao Yan asked in a low voice, there are so many peaks in front of him, if you take the wrong path, it will take a lot of time, and the atmosphere is full of things that Xiao has never heard of. This is the most strange thing.

"Senior Five-legged Golden Crow's memory is vague, and the fluctuations emanating from the Seal of Samsara are also somewhat obscure, but there seems to be a voice guidance in the Seal of Samsara. It should be right here."

Xun'er raised her head and looked at the land of ice and snow in the distance, a faint voice calling to her from the back of her mind.

"Then go to the ice and snow mountain first." Xiao Yan said in a deep voice, but when his eyes glanced over this huge ice and snow mountain, the glazed holy flame in his body also throbbed.

Like cheers, but also like fiery!

"Shhhhh."

With a sound, the three of them turned into a stream of light and stepped directly into the ice-snow mountain peak.

The void here is as pure as day all day long. Ice and snow are rampant in the glacier mountain range. Snow-capped snowflakes fall at random. There are traces of strange white mist emerging between the fall, which fills the entire mountain with an icy coldness. A bone-chilling chill.

In this land of ice and snow, even the blood flow in the body has become extremely slow, not to mention the operation of the power of reincarnation in the body, which is also greatly restricted.

"There are no human beings in the ice and snow peaks, and this is also one of the few forbidden places in the entire Tianfeng Territory."

"Because this kind of place has too terrifying restrictions on our power, no creature has ever stepped into this place rashly since ancient times."

"There used to be elders in our clan who came here to find out, but ended up in a bleak end."

In front of Feng Qian, the energy Feng Ying crawls to protect the three, reducing the huge cold power for them.

"Breakthrough!"

In an instant, an extremely crisp cry rang out in the world of ice and snow.

The sound of the cry was extremely strange, and it was impossible to tell which direction it came from, or what kind of creature it was making. It was just at the moment when the sound fell, the wind and snow in the whole world suddenly became denser, Xiao Yan and the others. His body and feet were covered with layers of thick white ice and snow.

"We must speed up and get through here."

"Step into the center of the snow-capped peak and have a look. If it doesn't work, then evacuate here."

Xiao Yan shook his head, his keen soul power made Xiao Yan clearly discover some clues.

With this level of yin and cold power, even the holy flame is helpless?

In the same way, with Xiao Yan's power, he was surprised to find that after using Fen Jue's power, he couldn't absorb the energy of heaven and earth here!

It seems that this gloomy cold air is slowly spreading, slowly swallowing all the creatures that step into the ice and snow peaks!

The combination of white snow and howling hurricanes made the three of them look extremely heavy. If they stayed here for a long time, I am afraid they would turn into a real frozen bone sooner or later.

"Brother Xiao Yan, it's impossible."

"It goes on like this..."

After walking for an unknown distance, everyone's consciousness began to become blurred little by little, and the cold feeling was transmitted, and there were bursts of ice threads on the hands and feet.

"Damn..."

"go back!"

Xiao Yan's situation was not very good at this time, and the situation here far exceeded their estimates.

"Wait a minute... There seems to be a familiar atmosphere here."

"Bang bang bang!"

When the three of them turned around and left, the artifact bracelet under Kaoru's hand buzzed, and the five-legged golden crow stepped on the golden flame and appeared.

Just appeared, even with its strength, he could smell a long-lost cold, but the huge beast was staring at the deep ice and snow world ahead.

In the world of ice and snow, the deeper you go, the more oppressive and cold you feel.

"Bang bang bang!"

The five-legged golden crow spewed out, and the golden flame pierced the sky and bloomed in this ice and snow world, but it was completely annihilated without bringing up such a huge momentum along the way.

"Brother Xiao Yan, what do you think that is?"

Following the action of the Five-legged Golden Crow, Xun'er followed the sensing of the divine weapon, suddenly raised her finger and pointed to a huge ice mark that appeared not far in front of her due to the attack of the Five-legged Golden Crow.

"The shape of these ice marks is very strange... Could it be?"

Feng Qian stared with both eyes, and with the help of the map in her hand, her beautiful eyes shrank slightly, and then she stared at the void above the ice marks After a while, the huge ice marks were like cracks in the world of ice and snow. With a huge opening, countless ice and snow began to surge, and even the void below the ice marks became distorted.

A snowy creature slowly appeared in their sight. After it appeared, everyone's breathing became extraordinarily dignified.

It seems that there is an extremely cold power that begins to be transmitted from the bottom of the ice mark!

"Is this a human?"

"Or what creature?"

Xiao Yan's breathing became quicker, but his soul power was unable to penetrate the cover of these blizzards. He could only vaguely see the creatures under the ice marks, which sometimes turned into human appearances, and sometimes turned into white giants. Waiting for the figure to turn out to be similar to Phoenix?

"This is……"

"Ice Phoenix???"

"That's... a mourner?"

Just when Xiao Yan and the three were struggling, the five-legged Golden Crow beside him lost his voice.

Everyone resisted the blizzard, and with the help of their peripheral vision, there was a huge creature in the ice and snow world that collapsed under the ice mark. On its back, there seemed to be a lonely figure standing proudly!

Under the blessing of the two, that kind of power is enough to truly prestige this world!
