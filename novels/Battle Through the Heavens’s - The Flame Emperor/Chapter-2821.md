"With the peak strength of the eighth-level reincarnation, you can burst out such combat power... You are the first person under the ninth level among the powerhouses who have fought against Ben Taishang since ancient times."

"Wait... this volatility?"

Feeling the unusually dazzling light on Long Feng's legs, countless dragon powerhouses shrank their eyes tightly, and a deep fear appeared in their bloodlines and even in the depths of their souls.

The dragon blood flowing in their bodies made them instantly distinguish the source of that power.

The keel, the supreme existence in the dragon family.

"Long Feng, why do you have a keel with your mere half-step nine-fold reincarnation strength?"

"The keel is among our dragon clan, and the reward can only be obtained after the strength reaches the quasi-nine reincarnation level!"

At this moment, the pupils of the Fire Dragon King, who was standing on the ground in the distance, condensed slightly, watching Long Feng's legs flickering with endless dim light. Among them, a section of yellow bare bones the size of a wrist was lying quietly in it, exuding a superiority. The terrible breath of all things.

The yellow bare bones are exactly the same as the bones of their legs, and they fit perfectly, making the defense of the latter's legs extremely strong, and even looming, exuding a terrifying aura!

"keel?"

Listening to the voice of the Fire Dragon King, Cailin and Yunyun on the side couldn't help but look at each other. These two words are undoubtedly unfamiliar to them.

Although I don't know what the so-called keel is, but with the intelligence of the two women, it is natural to know... The reason why Long Feng can defend against Xiao Yan's many holy flames, and even the attacks of the semi-divine rank fighting skills, I am afraid There is an inescapable relationship with this thing.

"Is this the reward from the Silver Dragon King to you?"

At this moment, Xiao Yan's face also showed a dignified expression, and he immediately spoke to Long Feng who was arrogant in front of him.

When looking at the yellow keel of the latter's legs, the blood of the Golden Dragon King in Xiao Yan's body became more and more violent, and there was a feeling of being ready to come out, as if he wanted to swallow the latter's keel!

The power of the blood of the Golden Dragon King is probably far more powerful than this keel... It's just that Xiao Yan has not yet completely controlled the blood of the Golden Dragon King. If it is forcibly released, the power that destroys the sky and destroys the earth is enough to stir Xiao Yan's body. The world was turned upside down, and the consequences were unimaginable!

"It's really worth it... But the Silver Dragon King will be punished sooner or later."

Looking at the yellow keel that fits perfectly in Long Feng's legs, the Dragon King Baxia's complexion is slightly solemn.

There have long been rumors in the great world that the value of the keel is almost as precious as the holy flame, but unfortunately no one knows that for the dragon creatures, the value of the keel is even higher than everything!

A keel can make its combat power rise countless times!

In the first World War, the Golden Dragon King, the supreme powerhouse of the Dragon Clan, only had the strength of the peak of the Nine Reincarnation Rank, but with the strength of the Dragon Clan keel and the power of the Dragon God Transformation, the fighting power that erupted was comparable to the demigod rank, which was enough to match the power of the Dragon Clan. The leader outside the territory is evenly matched!

This is the terrible thing about the keel.

"Win the king and lose the bandit, why talk so much nonsense?"

"This keel was transformed after the fall of Lord Tianyu Longzun. Its defensive power and destructive power cannot be shaken by your power."

"Wait for the success of our Lord, and everything will become a certainty."

Long Feng said, his pupils were already full of indescribable madness.

The legs flickered again, and a powerful dragon's might that shook the sky and earth broke out, causing the bodies of countless dragon powerhouses around to sink slightly.

Under such coercion, only the nine dragon kings were not threatened, and the fighting spirit in the bodies of the other powerhouses quickly faded, and even no sign of resistance was lifted.

"Tianyulongzun is one of the strongest among our dragon clan... At the beginning of the first world war, his strength reached the quasi-nine reincarnation level, and after his fall, he was turned into a keel. I didn't expect it to be ruined by you."

"Ugh."

At this moment, there is a thick anger rising in the eyes of the Fire Dragon King, not jealous, but an endless anger.

The value of the keel is too expensive, and for the dragon family, it is an extremely rare existence with one less and one less. Its birth is too harsh. Looking at the keel on Long Feng in front of him, the two dragon kings under Baxia were also heartbroken.

"Two seniors, is there no other way?"

Looking at Long Feng, who looked like a world-destroying dragon, the latter's legs swelled a little bit, and the power of the keel flickered, full of destructive aura and power, and even Xiao Yan's breathing became dignified.

"Maybe the power of holy flame gathered from all of you is enough to break through his defenses..."

"With his prudence, he won't give me the time to gather dozens of holy flames."

Facing the Fire Dragon King's idea, Xiao Yan directly shook his head and denied it.

Although Long Feng also has the pride of being the Supreme Elder of the Dragon Clan, but now the battle of the Dragon Tomb is imminent, once the army is destroyed, the fate of them and others will be irreversible.

Therefore, at such a moment of life and death, he will not take desperate risks. What he needs is foolproof. As long as he guards the entrance to the dragon tomb, the Silver Dragon King's plan will not be far from success.

As Xiao Yan's voice fell, the eyes of the strong men under the leadership of the Dragon King and the others gradually filled with resentment and unwillingness... Could it be that Cangtian really wants to kill the dragon clan?
