# Chapter Vol 4 2555: The evil artifact, the 7 beam beam formation!

"The soul power of the Divine Movement Realm?"

Looking at the seven white-eyed eyes gushing out from the face of the Seven-eyed Evil God in front of him, the spiritual palace in Xiao Yan's body swayed violently, and an unprecedented sense of unease permeated.

This guy's strength has reached the sixth level of reincarnation!

In addition to the mysterious power of angels, evil power, and even the soul power of the immortal wandering realm, Xiao Yan's pressure was greatly increased.

"Seven eyes... the power!"

Just after finishing speaking, strands of white flames appeared one after another in the blank face of the Seven-eyed Evil God.

Above the white flames, there are countless foul-smelling and corrupt demonic energy, slowly eroding everything in this world.

"Buzz!"

The seven eye patterns surrounded by flames slowly fell off and suddenly surrounded Xiao Yan!

The power of the seven-eye pattern turned out to be an ancient formation that slowly built a light attribute!

At the moment when it was surging, the burning determination in Xiao Yan's body, and even the manipulation and operation of forces such as the Dragon God Transformation, instantly returned to calm!

This kind of formation was different from the Dou Formation Master, and Xiao Yan could not break it, because the formation of the Angel Kingdom was used to restrict a single enemy from displaying fighting skills or strength, which was completely different from the formation used by Dou Formation Masters to resist thousands of enemies.

Weakness, daze, and despair poured into my heart little by little. Those strange and ferocious emotions, combined with the Seven Lights Array, slowly eroded and devoured Xiao Yan's will.

Xiao Yan is like a king in the fire with endless flame holy energy, imprisoned ruthlessly in a barren land.

There is no way to crack, there is no means to escape, all that can be done seems to be just waiting for the eternal death.

Humans, after all, are flesh and blood. Without a body, even a powerful person can only protect their soul from being damaged. In that case, their own strength will also be greatly reduced.

Not to mention that the attack of the Seven-eyed Evil God is mixed with the unique power of the kingdom of angels!

Form a formation with the unique power of the kingdom of angels, interfere with the thoughts and powers that limit the enemy's fighting skills!

Today's Xiao Yan, in addition to being able to mobilize the power of the seven holy flames, it seems that all the fighting skills in the body have become unfamiliar for a while, and he is deeply shrouded in the formation. Unleash the power of fighting.

If a five-layer reincarnation-level powerhouse does not have the blessing of fighting skills, what is the difference between him and ordinary people.

"This is the power of my evil artifact, specially prepared for you... The Seven Souls and Eyes of the Seven Lights Array."

Seeing Xiao Yan bound in place by the formation like a criminal, the Seven-eyed Heretic God burst into laughter crazily.

His entire body was shaky, and there was darkness on his face, without any outline, like a faceless man, gloomy and infiltrating.

As powerful as Xiao Yan, he was not worth a single blow.

"I will use this strongest blow to make you clearly realize... what a stupid decision to provoke us!"

"Dong, dong, dong!"

The Seven-eyed Evil God began to sing. He lost his face. He didn't know where he uttered his voice. However, the energy in the sky around him was gathering towards his body little by little at this time.

That is the power of angels, the purest power of angels!

The Seven-eyed Evil God actually wanted to use the forbidden technique of light to kill Xiao Yan!

Because he knew that Xiao Yan, who was carrying the seven kinds of holy flames, had almost reached an incomprehensible level of resistance to evil forces. Only the purest forbidden technique of light could cause real harm to Xiao Yan!

Although he wanted to temporarily deprive him of his evil power, he didn't care anymore.

As long as he can obliterate Xiao Yan, the descendant of the Xiao clan left by the Xiao clan, he can be considered worthy of Liu Hui's last wish... As for Xuanhe, the **** of poison, this guy will pay the price sooner or later.

"Boom!"

The energy was gathering, and the body of the Seven-eyed Evil God suddenly poured into the top of the Tianshan Demon Vein, and his hands made a gesture of supporting the sky, which was in sharp contrast to Xiao Yan who was suppressed by the Seven-eyed Beam Formation below.

"Damn... If this goes on, once the Light Forbidden Technique comes, I'm afraid this body of mine will be completely destroyed."

Xiao Yan's eyes became calm, looking at the flame shackles surrounding his body, the seven holy patterns and the power of holy flame, his eyes slowly became cold.

This body is the best body for cultivating Dragon God Transformation, and Xiao Yan will not give it up easily unless it is a last resort.

Today's Xiao Yan, unable to break through the shackles of the formation, unable to perform fighting skills, can only use the fusion of holy flames and holy patterns that have already been condensed on his body to fight against the seven-eyed evil spirit.

"Tear!"

Just when the two of them were condensing their monstrous offensive, the sky was instantly awakened by a slight tearing sound.

"Xiao Yan!"

The three girls took the lead in emerging from the endless clouds, and their beautiful eyes glanced at Xiao Yan, who was deeply trapped in the formation.

"Don't come here, be careful!"

Seeing the appearance of the three girls, Xiao Yan just wanted to speak out, but he saw the Tianshan demon veins surging in front of him in an instant, and the pitch-black energy viciously stirred the world!

Before the three women's figures could be stabilized, they were dragged into a dark cage of evil energy by the Demon Vein of Tiantian Mountain!

A burst of fear, the fear from the depths of the soul permeates!

Pitch-black energy, like the evil giant claws of thousands of feet, huge in size, with monstrous evil power, as if a single blow can completely annihilate the entire Li Soul Realm!

"What kind of power is this?"

"Hahaha!"

"Xiao Yan, today this is your heavenly tomb!"

"Let you all enjoy this scene of prosperity."

The Seven Lights Beam Formation surged slightly, releasing the purest halo, burning Xiao Yan's skin little by little, imprisoning his soul.
