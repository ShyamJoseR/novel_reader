# Chapter Vol 4 2727: Yanling Heavenly Tomb!

The evil shadow entrenched around the canyon was easily eliminated, Xiao Yan moved directly with the three daughters.

The four of them all jumped and jumped directly into the abyss of the canyon. For a time, the power in everyone's body seemed to fail in an instant.

The darkness drowned the four of them little by little. Although they felt dazed and uneasy at this time, under the guidance of the Book of Samsara, the four of them fell into the abyss without turning back.

"Is it really here... Why does the holy flame in the body convey a disturbing fluctuation?"

"Could it be a conspiracy of an alien race?"

In the process of descending, the corners of Mu Yingying's lips were dry, and even the many holy flames in her body instinctively released a very complicated wave.

There are uneasiness, joy, sadness and other strange emotions that fluctuate, which makes the latter's body tense. Make yourself uneasy.

"thump."

I don't know how long it has descended, but only a slight sound was heard. The four of them were like a strange and uninhabited realm. After passing through a peculiar thick and dark barrier, they successfully left the dark abyss and poured into the bottom. The new world after the end of the canyon.

In the process of penetrating the mysterious barrier, Xiao Yan and Su Xueying were the most dangerous. Their bodies, blood, and even their souls were all wandering around by the power contained in their barrier, and their whole minds trembled. It seems that there is some kind of transcendent power in the enchantment checking whether they are qualified to step under the enchantment.

That kind of investigation could not be avoided. If it weren't for the power of Xiao Yan's bloodline and the reincarnation seal on Su Xueying's eyebrows, I am afraid that a few people would have been pushed out by this barrier, or killed by it.

After breaking through this mysterious barrier and entering the world below, the pupils of the four were moved, and they couldn't help but be shocked.

Below the abyss of the canyon, there is no trace of evil, because this is a whole new world.

Just arriving in this new world, everyone stepped into the air and looked from a distance. In the hazy and illusory world below, Xiao Yan vaguely felt a great coercive force permeating the void. Although the coercion did not have a strong destructive power, it made Xiao Yan feel a tremendous pressure that had never been seen before.

According to Xiao Yan's guess, with such a huge pressure, even a peak powerhouse of the eighth-fold reincarnation cannot possess such a great coercive force!

The line of sight changed, and the world below became clear.

This is a huge mountain range that is slightly barren. There is no grass in the mountain world, and the surroundings are in a mess. There are many shadows of broken soldiers. There are signs that there have been several violent confrontations here. These traces are all battlegrounds. Keep.

After a brief inspection, the four of them looked extremely solemn. After all, the foreign evil race had already taken the lead in capturing the Samsara Sea. Whether this place has already been affected by it is already unknown.

"Strange...why did Jiu Xuan Huo Master and Xiao Lingshan's Reincarnation Seal become hot together."

"Could it be that their place of reincarnation is also here?"

Looking at Xiao Yan with a serious face beside him, and looking at the coordinates of the book of reincarnation in the latter's hand, this is clearly the reincarnation cemetery of the first elder Xiao Yan, why does the reincarnation of the other two strong men also emit such strange fluctuations?

Just stepping here, the four of them stood quietly in the corner of the mountain range, and did not act rashly, but Su Xueying, who was thoughtful, guessed something through the reincarnation light wave between the eyebrows of the other two women.

This area is mysterious, and I am afraid that there is a murderous intention inside. After a while, Xiao Yan was still, but his eyes began to move. After a while, he keenly captured a very hidden heaven and earth formation, which surrounded and enveloped the whole world. Even this formation was slightly dilapidated at this time, but Xiao Yan was able to know from some faint traces that this formation was undoubtedly a very powerful ancient formation!

Anyone who can mobilize or use such a formation is the true overlord of this world!

However, according to Feng Wan'er and other information about the Xiao clan's great elder, the latter did not seem to be proficient in fighting formations, which really made Xiao Yan feel a little concerned.

"Is this formation protecting this world?"

"But who did it?"

Although there was some doubts in his heart, Xiao Yan did not focus all his attention on it.

Xiao Yan's eyes swept away again, and the huge perception suddenly penetrated through the layers of mountains, avoiding the restraint and isolation of many powers, and probed deeper.

At the top and core of this mountain range, there is always some fairy mist that covers it, as if to hide some unknown secrets, but in front of Xiao Yan's soul power, it is all in vain. .

Looking up and observing, at the top core of this mountain range, among those mysterious clouds, there is a majestic and magnificent building standing quietly, and there is a sense of breathless coercion on it. emanated from it.

Outside the main hall, there are actually two endless abyss, slowly gushing out, and around the main hall at the top of the mountain range, there is a river like the Si River, which is isolated from the sky.

The water in the river is even more bottomless, the color is like a fiery red flame, and it is generally scorching hot. The core of the entire mountain range is extremely mysterious.

One hall, one river, and two caves occupy the core of this mountain range.

At such a strange time, the reincarnation mark on Su Xueying's eyebrows, which was far away from the core of the cloud and mist in the mountains, fell off instantly, and flew away uncontrollably towards the gate of the hall in the cloud and mist.

Xiao Yan was just about to make a move, but he saw the ray of light surging from the seal of reincarnation, and instantly transformed into a palm-sized fire talisman, heading towards the groove above the temple gate.

In the temple gate, there is a groove made of stacked red stones, and the pattern in it seems to be exactly the same as the reincarnation fire talisman that fell off Su Xueying's eyebrows!

"The seal of reincarnation...is it the key to open the hall?"

Seeing this scene Xiao Yan felt a sigh in his heart, and the power of his bloodline surged in an instant, which made the latter burst into ecstasy, a kind of blood boiling.

According to the instructions from the Book of Samsara and Su Xueying's Reincarnation Seal, in this hall, the place where the Great Elder Xiao Yan sleeps, the Yanling Heavenly Tomb!

It is said that even the experts of the nine-fold reincarnation cannot spy on the mysterious Yanling Heavenly Tomb, which contains endless mysteries!

"Is it really here?"

Seeing such a mysterious and strange scene, Xun'er and Mu Yingying looked at each other. The three Samsaras naturally needed to take the Great Elder Xiao Yan as the top priority... After all, the latter is the most powerful in the entire Samsara Sea. Strong man!

Once he is rescued, whether it is Xiao Lingshan or the reincarnation of the Nine Profound Fire Lord, it will naturally not be a problem.
