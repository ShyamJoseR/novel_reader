With the intelligence of the people in the kingdom of angels, if they were to judge Xiao Yan directly, they would probably not succeed. After all, Xiao Yan was the existence that the Queen of Angels personally named and protected.

But now that the kingdom of angels is in civil strife, it may be able to achieve a good effect by doing the opposite.

"What is this guy's purpose...what is it?"

Looking at the figure of Mo Yu hurriedly leaving, Ai Xi and Xiao Ke looked at the crystal picture quietly standing in the void, and their expressions could not help but become hesitant.

This power in Xiao Yan's body is clearly the same as the evil power that the Angel Kingdom hates the most. If this is known to the powerhouses of the Slaughter Angel lineage, it will probably cause an uproar in the entire Angel Kingdom.
