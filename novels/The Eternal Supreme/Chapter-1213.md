The countless patterns on the cauldron of mountain and river were activated. They shot out thousands of rays of light and painted the red world with a bright white.

Yunxiao kept performing incantation gestures with both hands, using the projection of the cauldron of mountain and river as a furnace to refine it.

With a flip of his hand, he took out the remaining Dragon shell from last time and threw it out.

These were all the rare treasures he had plundered from the palace of Donghai. He glanced at them one by one and pointed his two fingers continuously. One after another, the strange items flew into the projection of the cauldron of mountain and river in the lava.

After throwing over a hundred treasures, Yunxiao waved his hand, and a beam of light swept across the sky, collecting all the treasures.
