In the deep valley, there are many dangers. Even Xiao Yan’s current strength is a little tricky, but there is a vague intuition in his heart telling himself that the fight here seems to be carefully planned by someone here, as if guarding Some unknown secrets in general.

"Crisis chain after chain..."

Feeling the strangeness in the deep valley, Xiao Yan's complexion became calm, the demons in his body, the power of the god-slaying ruler, etc. began to flicker little by little.

In such an extremely dangerous place, Xiao Yan did not dare to have any reservations. After all, if he was not careful, although he would not fall here, he would stay here forever.

With the help of Sheng Yan's weak flame light, Xiao Yan moved cautiously in the deep valley. The Emperor Yan Ring and his own blood power slowly surrounded him, as if they were blocking the approach of those strange auras.

"This is... a formation?"

After a while, Xiao Yan vaguely saw a slightly raised rocky area in front of him. Upon closer inspection, it seemed to be a formation!

There was a dead silence around the formation, and there was no life, but in the area surrounded by the formation, there was a dark cave like a cave... From there, a hot sense of calling kept pouring out.

With this guess in his heart, many forces in Xiao Yan's body suddenly boiled.

Walking around carefully, it is not difficult to find that this is a triangle-like formation with a range of about tens of meters. The three sharp corners of the triangle formation seem to have a mysterious disc emerging from each.

There was nothing in the disc, but with Xiao Yan's keen perception, he vaguely felt that the arrangement on the disc was strange, as if it was used to hold something...

That kind of thing is probably the key to entering the cave!

"tear!"

At the moment when Xiao Yan hesitated, in the dark valley all around, there seemed to be some ferocious beasts awakened by Xiao Yan, and a terrifying aura that was enough to shake Xiao Yan slowly filled the air.

And from the traces of those shadows, I am afraid there is more than one end!

"Boom!"

"The power of holy flame!"

"The blood of the Xiao family!"

"The power of burning!"

The change in his eyes, combined with the change in the power in his body, Xiao Yan shouted at the same time, three groups of power flashed in his body, and it turned out that the three discs were instantly filled up.

In an instant, a dazzling ray of light shone up on this valley-bottom world.

At this moment, all the aura of crisis in the deep valley seemed to vanish in an instant.

Looking around, what kind of Jedi is here... The layout of the entire deep valley is rigorous, and it suddenly has the same effect as their Xiao clan ancestral hall!

The deep valley here... Actually there is a faint fluctuation of the same origin of the Xiao clan?

"The gate of heaven is open."

In an instant, an ancient whisper that seemed to have survived countless years, unexpectedly emerged from the strange formation in front of him.

A burst of glowing light flickered, and Xiao Yan was easily swallowed into it. As for the aura of the strange creatures entrenched in this place, the moment they followed Xiao Yan into the cave, they were annihilated by the power of the formation and vanished into ashes.

"I have waited for you to reincarnate in a hundred lives, and finally I have waited for you... the descendants of the Xiao family."

And when Xiao Yan swept into the cave, a long-lasting turbid low tone actually slowly dissipated into the deep valley... With the disappearance of this sound, all the remaining power in the entire deep valley completely disappeared.

It seems that no matter how powerful it is, under this kind of power, there is only a sad ending... The extraordinary divine power filled in this voice is too terrifying.

Without the power of darkness and the terrifying power of danger, the deep valleys here are no different from ordinary valleys. The only difference is that the valleys are filled with countless corpses and debris, including traces of humans and dragons and phoenixes. .

There are many dangers in such a restricted area of ​​life, but the opportunities that are full of them are enough to make countless strong people jealous... It's a pity that they are not as lucky as Xiao Yan.

"This is?"

After pouring into the cave, Xiao Yan felt the world spinning for a while, and then he sank into a pure white world.

This world is like an illusion, and there is not even a trace of heaven and earth energy around. At first glance, the majesty of the ancient spirit lingers in Xiao Yan's heart, and he is awe-inspiring!

In the world in front of him, there was only a dark giant with tens of thousands of feet in front of Xiao Yan.

This behemoth covered the sun and seemed unable to detect the true face of the latter with sight and perception, but Xiao Yan was able to clearly sense that on top of this behemoth, there was a sound that made Di Yanjie buzzing. Ancient waves.

Such obscure fluctuations are like finding their long-lost master.

"Shhhhhh!"

The Emperor Yan Ring does not have a spirit, but at this time, it is like an instinct, slowly pushing Xiao Yan's body, like that, it is clear that he wants Xiao Yan to climb the sky and reach the end!

Fen Jue, Flame God Cauldron, even Di Yan Jie, Holy King, there seems to be some mysterious connection between these four.

"Imprisoned the void... Unable to use any power of flight."

Walking slowly, I sensed an aura that shouldn't belong to this world, imprisoning everything here.

It seems that in the strange world here, you can only act according to the rules of this breath.

Without any hesitation, Xiao Yan stepped forward and climbed directly along the edge of the behemoth.

Although his appearance and speed were very embarrassing, when his body was attached to this behemoth, the nine-colored holy flames, Zhu Shenqi, and even Fen Jue in Xiao Yan's body became strangely quiet... as if their power Temporarily isolated and sealed by it!

What I can rely on now is only my own physical strength and the power of the Dragon God Transformation!

The body trembled, and a cyan flame suddenly separated from Xiao Yan's body, and immediately turned into a log surrounded by cyan flames, which turned out to be directly fit with this pitch-black pattern!

"Could it be... this is the Flame God Cauldron?"
