# Chapter Vol 4 2728: 3 Tomb Symbiosis!

"Boom!"

The gate of the temple entrenched in the core of the mountain, with the surging of the reincarnation mark on Su Xueying's eyebrows, slowly opened like the place of the god's residence that had been sealed with countless reincarnations.

The shocking and magnificent sound, accompanied by an unusually harsh sound of gears, slowly resounded throughout the world.

The four people flickered and came to the top of this mountain range in an instant. With the help of some perception power, they slowly probed the so-called Yanling Heavenly Tomb.

In the mausoleum of the main hall surrounded by clouds and mist, there is no end at all. The entire mausoleum is like an endless abyss world, more like in a chaotic void, the space around it is shattered, even the one above the mausoleum hall. The two black holes also have endless whistling power.

The river outside the core mausoleum hall is even more mysterious, but now it has the reincarnation light path paved by the reincarnation seal, filling it up, temporarily unable to form an obstacle.

Inside the mausoleum hall, there is a lot of wreckage, and there are many extremely deformed bones, shards of broken soldiers, etc., and the four voids in the entire mausoleum are all tragic traces left after fierce battles.

Even with Xiao Yan's huge perception, it is not difficult to find that on these bones and corpses, there are actually symbols of many extraterritorial royal families and other identities!

The entire hall was filled with dead silence, but in such a quiet state, Xiao Yan and the four instinctively sensed a terrifying crisis.

The most terrifying thing was that in the depths of the mausoleum hall, a faint force of power swept through it, making Xiao Yan feel like a great enemy, and the pressure was greatly increased... It seemed that the ethereal aura at the end was this. The only master of the film world!

Even now Xiao Yan faced such oppression, his heart palpitated!

That kind of power is really too powerful. Even if he uses Yanshen Cauldron and many other powers, Xiao Yan's chance of winning against the latter is probably less than 10%!

"You stay here on guard, I'll go in and take a look."

"If the Great Elder sleeps here, I will definitely wake him up."

After a stalemate for a while, Xiao Yan spoke to the three women beside him with a solemn expression...

Although they have reincarnation, their current strength has not yet succeeded in reincarnation, and naturally they do not have the strength to deal with the current situation. Although Xiao Yan could not bear to let them do it himself, after all, this situation is too dangerous, and Xiao Yan cannot protect himself. Inevitably increasing the difficulty.

"No, this matter is very important, I have to follow Brother Xiao Yan."

Xiao Yan's voice just fell, but Xun'er on the side made a direct voice, her words were decisive, and Xiao Yan's kindness was cut off abruptly.

No matter what happened in the past, now that Reincarnation is full of crises, she would not let Xiao Yan take risks alone.

"Although the strength of the three of us is not as good as you, but with the cooperation, the strength should not be underestimated."

"And this trip is so hard, it is naturally impossible to return home."

Mu Yingying's delicate body stood up, and she naturally agreed with Xun'er's idea. As one of the mistresses of the Endless Fire Territory, how could she not be able to help Xiao Yan.

"In that case, let's go, don't waste time."

Su Xueying turned slightly and looked at the two women with envy.

"There are many dangers in the mausoleum hall. After entering it, everything must be arranged by me."

"Okay."

As soon as the voice fell, the four of them trembled slightly and rushed towards it cautiously. When they had to cross the mysterious river, if it wasn't for the help of the power of Samsara, I am afraid that the four of them would face a very terrifying crisis.

"Sir, do you see?"

And just as the four of them poured into the mausoleum hall, a mighty army of black shadows appeared in this world, but their black robes were all dripping with blood, apparently just to break through the strange magic knot. The world has spent a lot of energy.

"Don't worry, wait for them to wake people up, and then we will take advantage of the fisherman."

"Is this eight-fold reincarnation level peak strength, can these guys run away?"

Facing such an appearance, the Shuizhi Holy Throne smiled slightly, and the gloomy voice resounded throughout the world!

One of the three holy clans, the Shuizhi Holy Seat, the peak of the eighth-level reincarnation, such strength should not be underestimated.

"The Holy See is wise."

Hearing the response of the Shuizhi Holy Seat, all the evil shadows around them took action one after another, arranging endless evil traps in this world.

When Xiao Yan and the others stepped into the Great Hall of Yanling Heavenly Tomb, they felt an inexplicable coercion shrouding their bodies, as if they had fallen into a trap set up by others, unable to escape, unable to retreat, only Move forward cautiously.

Among the four, Xiao Yan and Su Xueying felt the most obvious.

Many powers in Xiao Yan's body have slowed down, and even his soul is slightly stunned. On the other hand, Su Xueying's pretty face is tense, and the reincarnation mark at the center of her eyebrows actually has a sense of tearing, which makes the latter prepare. suffer.

Stepping on the ground of the Yanling Heavenly Tomb, Xiao Yan ignored the changes around him and slowly raised his head to scan the world, trying to find the reincarnation of the Great Elder.

Looking up, the Yanling Heavenly Tomb was so huge and spectacular that the four of them had a feeling of insignificance like an ant.

No matter how many tragic traces of fierce battles have occurred in the mausoleum, there is no trace of evil here, and even a touch of ancient fluctuations slowly permeates this world, as if it is protecting something.

In the entire mausoleum, countless mountain peaks stand quietly, and there are countless complex patterns on the mountain peaks, as if they are sheltering a certain life. The bottomless dark cracks, in the cracks, the gloomy wind bursts, people can't help but sigh.

"What a brutal fight."

After a moment of hesitation, Mu Yingying's beautiful eyes stretched slightly, and her voice turned out to contain a touch of shock.

What a huge battle is needed to destroy this place as it is today, at least, the ordinary eight-fold reincarnation level may not be able to do it.

"The strength attributes of these mountain peaks are different, but they seem to be guarding something, and although the periphery of countless mountain peaks is not broken, the mountain peaks are miserable."

Xun'er also noticed something, and immediately spoke slowly.

"You... look!"

After a while, Su Xueying's icy pretty face was moved for a while, her fingers trembled as she raised her fingers, pointing to the endless high altitude of the many peaks, she lost her voice!

In the sky supported by countless mountain peaks, three white tombs of light looming like a mirage emerge.

"Three...three tombs of reincarnation?"

Looking at the white tomb of reincarnation that was almost perfectly integrated into the void, everyone discovered it after Su Xueying used the reincarnation seal to perceive it.

Countless peaks in this world surround the high sky behind, and there seem to be three groups of white reincarnation tombs quietly entrenched.

"The tomb of light with the largest volume is the one of the first elder."

"The other two... are Xiao Lingshan."

"And the Nine Profound Fire Lord."

At this moment, the three women simultaneously moved the reincarnation mark between their eyebrows, and a moment later, a rare joy appeared in their hearts.
