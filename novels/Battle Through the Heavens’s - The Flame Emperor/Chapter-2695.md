# Chapter Vol 4 2643: decision!

The vast magic sound easily emerged from the abyss of the Endless Fire Territory Star Continent, instantly attracting the attention of countless powerhouses!

The monstrous fire curtain composed of the flames of the Endless Fire Territory slanted towards the abyss like rain, trying to completely suppress and obliterate the latter.

It is a pity that the power of these holy flames is too cowardly for this figure.

Almost most of the endless fire curtain was completely annihilated in the hands of Mo Tuo, and the top executives in the endless fire domain were moved.

"This guy……"

Looking at the evil aura in the distance, the fluctuations contained in it even surpassed the powerhouses of the extraterritorial evil clan in the demon cloud outside the endless fire domain, the holy flame in Yun Yun's body buzzed, involuntarily from Floating out of the body.

The sacred holy flame trembled, bursting out with a terrifying aura that shook the world, and it contained a sense of danger.

The source of that danger came from the abyss in the distance. Obviously, the latter also felt a great pressure from Mo Tuo!

"This guy is the legendary Buddha world powerhouse that has been lost for a long time... Mo Tuo?"

Numerous high-level executives of the Endless Fire Territory all looked at the endless abyss of the Star Continent in the distance!

Two evil auras stand here, Maduo and Xuanyin Rakshasa!

At this time, Mo Tuo's slightly scarlet eyes appeared with endless fierce light, and through the void, the monstrous evil power swept out, shocking everyone present!

Especially the Xiao clan guardians on the endless platform, the pressure doubled, as if the source of this hatred and anger was them!

It was just that no one noticed that on the body of Mo Tuo, which was wrapped in a jet-black cassock, the scars that could not be healed were very conspicuous, and those scars seemed to be arranged in some kind of mysterious mark.

"The strong man in the Buddha world, the Buddha's younger brother, Mo Tuo... According to the rumors, wasn't he killed by Xiao Yan, the elder of the Xiao clan, during the war between heaven and earth?"

"How could he still be alive?"

"And according to the records in the ancient books, when they fought, his strength was only at the sixth level of reincarnation."

"But now... how can it be possible to break through to the eighth level of reincarnation!"

There was a dignified look on Yun Yun's pretty face. Regarding the matter of the Xiao Clan's Great Elder, the Guardians and the space intelligence dealers knew a lot.

But now looking at the living figure in front of her, Yun Yun still sighed.

The evil spirit that pervaded Mo Tuo's body made even her feel a little troubled. If he was allowed to swept away his anger, I am afraid that the entire endless fire field would be destroyed in an instant!

Huge shock and monstrous pressure swept through Yun Yun's heart little by little, and a daze filled her mind. It seemed that this situation was almost dead, and there was no way to solve it!

Under the crushing of absolute strength, all actions are just useless.

The Eighth Reincarnation Stage!

The power of Modo alone can completely wash the current endless fire domain, not to mention the evil power lingering in the demon cloud outside the endless fire domain!

But this is where Xiao Yan's hard work lies. Since Xiao Yan left him to take care of it before he left, even if he did everything he could, he would never let him destroy this place!

The powerhouses in the endless fire domain all around looked at the figure of Mo Tuo and the countless evil figures dormant in the surrounding demon cloud, and immediately looked at each other in dismay.

Despair began to emerge little by little in the hearts of everyone in the Endless Fire Territory, and sadness and tears were slowly eroding everyone here.

But among the hundreds of thousands of people, the tears were not the usual greed for life and fear of death, but were even full of fanaticism and unyielding arrogance!

It seems that for them, being able to become a member of the Endless Fire Territory is already an honor. Now that the Fire Territory is in trouble, the meaning of protecting the shortcoming in the hearts of these powerhouses is becoming more and more obvious.

Even if you sacrifice your life for this, you will not hesitate!

"Lord... Mistress."

Among the high-level people, there are many people with anger in their eyes, and they speak out directly. The strength of their bodies begins to rise, and the monstrous fighting spirit permeates the air.

Even if it was a deadly battle, they never complained!

The Endless Fire Territory has sheltered them for countless years, and now it is time for them to contribute to it.

"Well, I'm here."

On Yun Yun's face, there was a poignant and bleak smile, and she turned to look at the top of the endless fire domain behind her. The strength of these powerhouses, even if some were only at the level of fighting immortals, there was no trace of retreat and fear in their eyes.

She naturally believes in the loyalty of the high-level leaders behind her. They are definitely not people who are greedy for life and fear of death. As long as she gives an order, countless strong people will come forward bravely!

But so what?

Do you want them to sacrifice in vain?

Sacrifice them to protect the endless fire domain?

Naturally, these answers are all negative, because it seems that only Yun Yun, Tianlong, Earth and Phoenix can accurately sense the exact strength of each other in the huge endless fire realm!

What is the concept of the Eightfold Reincarnation Stage?

Even a seven-layer reincarnation-level powerhouse is only as small as an ant in his hands, not to mention the powerhouses around him who are mostly below the fifth-layer reincarnation level.

If you order it yourself, it will undoubtedly be a desperate attempt to completely kill the last hope of the Endless Fire Territory.

"If Xiao Yan was here, what would he do?"

There were thousands of thoughts in his heart, and the body of the demon in the distance began to shake, step by step into the endless abyss, and came towards the endless platform.

"Mu Feng, Yun Qi, Yuan Yan obey orders!"

"I will create an escape route for you later, and you will escape here with part of the bloodline power of the Endless Fire Territory... Remember, you only have three breaths of time."

After a while, Yun Yun's beautiful eyes slowly opened, and there was a look of determination in them. The divine sword and the holy flame were held in both hands, and the righteousness was awe-inspiring!

"Mother...why don't you fight them for life and death?"

Mu Feng's red eyes were filled with an unprecedented madness.

Under such a catastrophe, no one has any desire to retreat, but why is the mistress?

The strength of the demon in front of him is unknown, but it has not yet reached this point.

"The remaining green hills are there, don't be afraid that there will be no firewood to burn."

Facing everyone's doubts, Yun Yun could only shake her head slightly.

Their strength is too different from the other party In addition to the fact that Moduo deliberately concealed it, they did not realize the true strength of Moduo.

And even if it is a deadly battle, with the power of the endless fire domain, no one can break through the siege!

A death battle is nothing but sacrifice in vain.

"Listen to my mistress's orders!"

"You and others escorted the Xiao Gui family, the guardian family, and some immature blood power out of here."

"Get ready."

"Buzz!"

Finally, a gloomy gloom appeared on Yun Yun's cheeks, the divine sword and the holy flame were fused and intertwined at the same time, and the beautiful light was filled with a terrifying energy that shook the world.
